Sorcerer. The newest of the five. She joined during the caravan arc in [[Session 05 - The Caravan]], shortly before the fighting started at the ford.

Sorcery is power that lives in the blood rather than power that is studied or granted, which makes her different in kind from the mages the party has been walking beside.

**The battle was her arrival.** She dropped a cloud of fog between [[Seraveth]] and his own mages, cutting the most dangerous man on the field off from every person whose job was to keep him alive. They could not see him, reach him, or help him. Then she put an arcane orb into him and helped run the mage who had overseen the entire march down to a single hit point.

She also killed [[Son Soleth]], after [[Zaff]] slowed him.

She bought petite light leather armour for 9 gold on the way out of [[Ghol Uzhak]], and she carries [[Seraveth's Lost Sceptre]], taken at Blood Gulch Ridge.

She is from the eastern forest, and she is on a journey to find her dream house. The party learned both in [[Session 07 - Free Until Tomorrow]], on the road into the very forest she came from.

In that session she charmed [[Eugene Johnson]] so thoroughly he turned red and called her too young for him, read the ruins of [[Rootfield]] and found a reluctance in the killing magic, saw the blue tether running from [[Scalybark]] into the sky, and produced five potions of flying and a broom of flying from her pack when nobody knew she had them. God works in mysterious ways. At Seedfall her mage hand reached for a seed and poked a paragliding gnome in the eye instead, and he went down somewhere out of sight.

When something must be rolled well, the party hands it to her. She is also, by her own accounting, kind of the reason they won the battle.

## Brand

**The Star Held in a Fist.** A four-pointed star gripped tight, light leaking between the fingers. The fire marked the containment rather than the power. When it appeared, [[Yagra Emberveil]] looked away from the mark and directly into her eyes for an uncomfortably long moment, and then said *"Hold tight, little fire."* Nothing more.
