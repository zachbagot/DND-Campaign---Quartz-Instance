Rogue. Also called Poppy. The party's way into any room that is locked.

**She has died once.** In [[Session 01 - The Burning of Briarwood]] a dwarf named Blonk rolled a natural 1 on a guiding bolt and killed her outright, then resurrected her. Nobody has asked her what happened in between.

In [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]] she dismembered the goblin prince [[Skarnick]], cut out his heart, and drove it into the wall of the cave. A shudder ran through the cave when she did it, and it has never been explained. She also took [[Viola]]'s eye in the ambush on the road.

Her best work is in [[Session 05 - The Caravan]]. She burgled two mage tents, came out with a journal and three copied pages of arcana she could not read, and then tricked [[Liss]] into casting Comprehend Languages on her so she could. That is how the party learned about the [[Death Rattles]].

She helped an orphanage in [[Aquitaine]] on an earlier visit, which is why [[Thrain Battleborn]] talked to her.

## Brand

**The Key of Smoke.** A skeleton key in curling smoke, its teeth dissolving at the edges. Not a mark of thievery. A mark of doors. See [[The Five Brands]].
