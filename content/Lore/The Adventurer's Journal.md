The journal that came out of the Traveler's Trove haul in [[Session 03 - Beef's Beefy Doors]], revealed as magical in [[Session 07 - The Druid's Ruins]].

**It writes the party's story as it happens.** Everything they live through appears in its pages, written as it progresses. It does not report what happens elsewhere, to other people, in places the party has never been. It knows what they lived and nothing else.

**It carries a map that fills in behind them.** The map shows the land, the largest cities in either direction, and every point the party has already been, appearing as they travel. It is a record of where they have been, not a guide to where they should go.

This wiki is that journal. Reading it is reading the book.
