The marks given to the party at the fire in [[Ghol Uzhak]] during [[Session 06 - Blood Gulch Ridge]], performed by [[Yagra Emberveil]].

## How it works

The shaman draws a coal from the fire with her bare hand. Her skin does not redden. When the coal touches skin there is no pain, only a profound cold and the smell of rain on hot stone. The mark surfaces from **beneath** the skin, like something rising through water, rather than being pressed in from above. It takes about three breaths.

[[Yagra Emberveil]] does not choose the marks and does not know in advance what is coming. She watched each one appear with genuine curiosity.

## The marks

### [[Throkk]] · The Broken Tusk Made Whole
A tusk snapped in two, the halves bound by a ring of flame. Among orcs a broken tusk is the mark of shame and exile. A mended one exists in no tradition anyone knows.

*Yagra went still for a long moment, then said quietly, not to him: "So. It does forgive." She would not elaborate. Old orcs may touch this mark without asking and walk away without a word.*

### [[Polly]] · The Key of Smoke
A skeleton key rendered in curling smoke, its teeth dissolving at the edges as if mid-vanish. Not a mark of thievery. A mark of doors.

*Yagra snorted. "Of course. Keep it away from my things." Then she patted Polly's hand once, which she did for no one else.*

### [[Zaff]] · The Seed in the Ash
A single seed cracking open, its first root descending into a bed of ash. Aasimar are creatures of the sky and of light coming down. This marks something that grows up out of burned ground.

*"The sky sent us a gardener. Good. The sky owes us."*

### [[St. Joseph]] · The Hearth With Two Shadows
A hearthfire casting two shadows in opposite directions. A priest carries his god with him, so he never stands anywhere alone. Two shadows also means two loyalties.

*"Two lights. Hm. When they argue, priest, and they will argue, stand still. Let them fight over you. Whichever one stays is yours."*

### [[Enya]] · The Star Held in a Fist
A four-pointed star gripped tight in a clenched hand, light leaking between the fingers. Sorcery is power born in the blood rather than earned or granted, and the fire marked the containment, not the power.

*Yagra looked away from the mark and directly into her eyes for an uncomfortably long beat. "Hold tight, little fire." Nothing more.*

## What it means

> *"Hear what you now are. Any hearth of the people is your hearth. Any orc who sees this mark owes you shade, water, and truth, and you owe the same, so do not make me regret you. You did not join the tribe tonight. Understand this. The tribe is not a thing you join. The fire simply stopped pretending you were strangers."*

## The open question

She said the fire had been expecting them.

She never said what for.
