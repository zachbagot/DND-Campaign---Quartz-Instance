The ruling body of [[Ilyara]]. The city has no king and no monarch, and when [[Zaff]] asked for one he was laughed at.

Each borough of the city sits under one petal of the great flower and takes its colors from it, and the boroughs together rule through the Council.

The Council's sprite scouts patrol the forest approaches. [[Aster]] and [[Sylvia]] are two of them, the first line watching the treeline, and it was their patrol that intercepted the party in [[Session 07 - The Druid's Ruins]] before it could walk into [[Varkesh]].

## The five seats

Named to the party by [[Wix]] and met in [[Session 08 - The Two Thrones]].

| Seat | Who holds it | What it carries |
|---|---|---|
| The Root | [[Maelis]], an elder forest treant of some five hundred years | Truth, history and the city's memory. She opens trials |
| The Bloom | [[Lethiel]], a dryad of six hundred | Growth, healing, and the children |
| The Thorn | [[Brindle]], a satyr | Defence, justice and punishment |
| The Wing | [[Pipri]], a sprite | Secrets and information |
| The Seed | [[Seln]], a young forest gnome sorcerer | Power, magical growth, the city's future, and part of its guard |

## How Ilyara tries someone

Not the way anywhere else does it. The accused is not asked to prove innocence.

> *"It's not a question of their innocence or guilty. It's a question of their consequence today."*

He has to convince the council of his **need to live**. Each councillor keeps a private reckoning that the accused never sees, and at the end the five are averaged. Low enough and he is executed, high enough and the consequence is something he can walk away from.

Testimony is open to anyone in the gallery, prosecution and defence both, and the crowd is not quiet. At [[Polly]]'s trial it chanted *eye for an eye* through most of the proceedings.

An execution here is carried out by the wronged party, handed a blade in front of the city. In [[Session 08 - The Two Thrones]], [[Tallow]] was handed one and put it down.
