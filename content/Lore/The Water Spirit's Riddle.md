# The Water Spirit's Riddle

Spoken by a water spirit that rose from the river and flooded the bridge on the approach to [[Aquitaine]] in [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]].

> *When moonlight crowns the child of none*
> *Yet all the kingdoms bow*
> *A silver thread through shadow spun*
> *Unseen upon her brow.*
>
> *Blood of thrones in silence kept,*
> *A name the stars conceal,*
> *Only the fire she has not met*
> *Can prove what fate made real.*
>
> *Seek the trial ancient kings endured*
> *Where truth and ember blend*
> *For royal blood is never sure*
> *Until the flames ascend.*

[[St. Joseph]] attacked it before it could say anything else. The spirit washed the entire party down the river, cost them their carriage, and left.

Nobody at the table has worked out who it was talking about.
