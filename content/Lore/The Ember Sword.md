A sword taken by [[Throkk]] from [[The Tomorrow Shop]] in [[Session 07 - Free Until Tomorrow]], free until tomorrow.

**It is as big as you want it to be.** At rest it is nothing but a hilt. Drawn, it is whatever size its wielder asks of it, and it is not metal at any size. The whole blade is flame, closer to a torch than a sword, and it scorches whatever it rests against. Throkk's first act as owner was to make it as big as he could physically hold and drag it behind him through a city built of living trees.

What is known about its history comes from [[Tallow]]: Throkk is the third person in a year to take an ember sword, and Tallow does not know what happened to the other two.

Nothing has been paid for it yet.
