# Prince Caelen Tidelorn

Son of [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]] and heir to [[Aquitaine]]. Sharp features, storm-blue eyes, and a cold calculating manner.

Beneath the polish is a bloodthirsty streak. He enjoys combat, intimidation, and any chance to prove dominance.

He holds a deep prejudice against anyone not of [[Aquitaine]], believing the coastal nobility superior and destined to rule. It sits just below the surface and comes out whenever he feels challenged.

He was betrothed to [[Celeste Hawthorne]], and on the day she arrived he looked past her at [[Seraphine]].
