An old forest gnome who keeps the dream shop in [[Ilyara]], among mounds of dirt, maps and cages. His name is on his name tag.

In [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]] he bought [[St. Joseph]]'s broom of flying and five potions of flying for 150 gold, after St. Joseph opened at 220. [[Throkk]] called him Bill, then Dylan, and showed him two eggs, which were seeds from the mother plant. The throbbing one, [[Zaff]]'s, he valued at two to five hundred gold, and ten times that fully grown. Throkk's, he thought, was dead.

> *"An egg becomes a chicken. A seed becomes a tree. An egg can die, so can a seed."*

He offered to plant and raise both for a share of the final price, and Throkk signed his great book with a flat line, as Throkk the orc. He also let [[Enya]] look through his maps, and warned her about the Red Boughs on the road to [[Varkesh]]: *"All those creepy skeletons hanging about."*
