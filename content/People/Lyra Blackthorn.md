A sarcastic half-elf rogue with short silver hair and amber eyes, confident and cunning, quick to mock and quicker to strike. She led [[Garin]] and [[Viola]] as an adventuring trio drinking at the Rusty Tankard in [[Session 01 - The Burning of Briarwood]], where all three rejected [[St. Joseph]] and turned hostile.

In [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]] the trio ambushed the party as bandits, trying to take [[Celeste Hawthorne]]. Lyra surrendered peacefully and was the only one taken captive.

[[Throkk]] argued she should be spared the stockades, and [[Mayor Aldric Hawthorne]] agreed. Her older brother [[Rylan]] remembered that, and it is the reason the party got a hearing with [[Beef the Bard]].

## Why they were waiting

She and her crew were standing outside the bar in [[Briarwood]] when [[Mayor Aldric Hawthorne]] begged the party to rescue his daughter. They heard where [[Celeste Hawthorne]] was, how far it was, and what the reward was worth. They did not need to track anyone. They waited for the party to do the work and tried to take her at the mouth of the cave.
