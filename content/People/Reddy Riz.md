A goblin child from [[Prince Krixen Ironfang]]'s camp in the forest north of [[Ilyara]]. His friends call him **the Rizzler**.

In [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]] he watched [[Zaff]] get named a prophet and could not stop staring at him afterward, grinning. His friend slipped the party a note, and then he introduced himself.

> *"My name's Reddy Riz, great to meet you guys. My friends call me the Rizzler."*

He protects himself with a butter knife. He asked [[Polly]] for a toy and got a mechanical caterpillar.

He followed the party into [[Ilyara]] and became [[St. Joseph]]'s best disciple. Sent out to pick the pockets of the town for God, he brought back 100 gold on his own. When the party tried to leave the children behind, he answered the prophet's call with a speech that turned every child in earshot around, and he went through the teleportation circle to [[Kharavel]] with three others. When St. Joseph proposed the disciples as a distraction in the coming siege, the Rizzler took out his butter knife.
