# Celeste Hawthorne

Daughter of Mayor Aldric of [[Briarwood]], and the reason the party left home.

She was taken by the goblins the dragon dropped in [[Session 01 - The Burning of Briarwood]], and the mayor offered part of her dowry to get her back. The party found the hideout and killed [[Skarnick]], the goblin prince who had her.

She was betrothed to [[Prince Caelen Tidelorn]], and the party escorted her to [[Aquitaine]] to complete the arrangement, along with her childhood friend [[Seraphine]].

On arrival both the Duke and the Prince mistook [[Seraphine]] for her.

She is still in [[Aquitaine]].
