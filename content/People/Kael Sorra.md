Orc scout and tracker, late twenties, five foot four. A childhood acquaintance of [[Throkk]].

In [[Session 05 - The Caravan]] he warned the party during a drinking game that an ambush was coming when the caravan crossed the river at the ford, and suggested they take action.

In [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]] he slipped the party a pouch mid-battle and joined the melee as an ally, with a whispered *"Vorrak is watching from the ridge, make your strikes look good."*

His father is Senn Sorra, an elder among the Sorath guides.

He does not know that [[Throkk]] killed the tax collectors whose deaths brought the humans down on their people. Nobody does.
