A tall, strikingly handsome human with a beautiful jawline and a smile he rarely puts away. A mage of the Mages Guild, and [[Prince Krixen Ironfang]]'s agent.

The party met him at midnight in the [[Petal and Pitcher]] in [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]]. He stood up from a corner table, embraced [[Polly]], and said he was so excited to meet the rest of the heroic party. **His red cloak looked very like [[Seraveth]]'s.** He recognised [[Enya]]'s sceptre, and remarked that the last person who held it did not fare so well.

He laid out Krixen's plan to take the throne of [[Varkesh]] from his father, and two ways to get an army for it: the mercenaries of [[Dur Soleth]] or the gnomes of [[Kharavel]]. Pressed by [[Zaff]] on why a mage spends years helping a goblin prince, he said his work is to serve the people, the Mages Guild, the order and the First Chair, *"whatever is necessary,"* and that he is not manipulating the goblins: *"We're empowering them to serve themselves."*

He teleported the party to [[Kharavel]] through a circle he painted himself outside the inn. When [[Throkk]] grabbed his cloak to pull him, it was like grabbing steel, and he did not move an inch. The palace guards in Kharavel greeted him with *oh, fuck you again*.

He will not be with the party when they go under [[Varkesh]]. He and Krixen are going for the Sovereign.
