An awakened tree in the square of [[Rootfield]], met in [[Session 07 - The Druid's Ruins]] when [[St. Joseph]] cast sacred flame at his base and he stood up out of the ground to ask why they had disturbed his slumber.

[[Zaff]] covered the priest's mouth and [[Polly]] explained they had been burning a fungus off his bark, which Scalybark accepted before settling his roots back into the earth.

He has been awake for only a few months, after wandering for centuries, and he remembers little. *"Time is strange in these woods, where you're awake and then you're asleep for generations."* He says the trees of the forest move because they feel the wind and the nature and move to it, and that he is his own being, for now at least.

[[Enya]] read him with arcana and saw a blue aura around him, and a tether running off him into the sky until it fades from sight.

Scalybark is one of his many names. It is the one he offered that day.
