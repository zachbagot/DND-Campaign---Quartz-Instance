Older brother of [[Lyra Blackthorn]]. A brooding half-elf ranger in a dark cloak with a permanent five-o-clock shadow.

Because the party spared his sister, he put their name forward to [[Beef the Bard]] in [[Session 03 - Beef's Beefy Doors]]. That introduction is the reason the party has any standing in [[Aquitaine]] at all.
