Orc resistance leader. Mid forties, six foot seven.

He planned the ambush at the ford that became the battle in [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]], and he watched the party choose a side from the ridge.

Afterward he did something no orc has done in ten thousand years. He invited outsiders into [[Ghol Uzhak]].

> *"I'm not going to thank you. Words are cheap. Three days east there's a marsh no map shows. Under it is Ghol Uzhak, the old den. The root of every tribe that's ever carried a banner. There's a fire there older than the worlds, and a woman who speaks for it. If it sees in you what I saw today, it will mark you, and that mark makes you tribe. Not guests. Tribe. No human has stood inside Ghol Uzhak since before your people had names. The elders will argue. Let them. You bled in the water with us."*

## The trade

After the battle the party had taken [[Captain Aldric Renn]] alive, and a captured human officer is worth a great deal to an orc resistance. Vorrak wanted him.

The party wanted [[Liss]] instead, a mage who had been decent to [[Polly]] on the march and was now in orc hands.

**He took the trade.** [[Liss]] walked free. [[Captain Aldric Renn]] is his prisoner now, held in the shanty settlement on the shore of the lake, outside the pocket dimension.

## At the fire

At the ceremony itself he led the party in and then pointedly stepped back and said nothing. It was not his ceremony.
