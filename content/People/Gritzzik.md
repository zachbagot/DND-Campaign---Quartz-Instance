# Gritzzik

A goblin in [[Aquitaine]] who sells his goods off a sheet spread on the ground in the market.

People accuse him of stealing. He gets bullied for being a goblin.

He wants to open a shop and call it **Gritzzik's Glorious Goods**.

The party walked past him in [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]] on their way up from the river.
