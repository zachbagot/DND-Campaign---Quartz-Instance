A pixie scout of [[The Council of Boroughs]], yellow-winged, married to [[Sylvia]]. The pair shadowed the party from the moment they entered the eastern forest in [[Session 07 - Free Until Tomorrow]], until [[Polly]] stealthed in behind them and startled the pollen off their wings.

He warned the party off [[Varkesh]] and guided them toward [[Illyara]] instead. When [[Polly]] let go of the flying chain mid-flight, Aster dove after him and broke his fall with a fresh kick of pixie dust.

He is [[Wix]]'s cousin. When not on scout duty, he and Sylvia paint flowers.

His wings have to stay damp or he will never fly again, which keeps him bound to the forest.
