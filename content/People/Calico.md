A satyr of [[Ilyara]] who testified against [[Polly]] at his trial in [[Session 08 - The Two Thrones]].

Her brother went into the woods the day before. She went after him and found his body opened in the same way [[Tallow]] had been opened that morning, which is why the city was willing to believe one stranger had done all of it.

She is one of the four deaths [[Polly]] was charged with and did not cause.
