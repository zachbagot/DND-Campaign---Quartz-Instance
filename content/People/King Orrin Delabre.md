The King of [[Kharavel]]. A very old rock gnome who carries himself like a soldier.

In [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]] he received the party in his throne room, an amphitheatre full of wealthy gnomes, and told them what he wanted from [[Varkesh]]: the lava flowing again, and the goblins back in the chasm as a workforce. He has lived many years in siege tents and those beds no longer suit his back, so his plan is subtlety, a great feint that marches his army at the goblin border and draws their army out without ever engaging.

[[Polly]] asked for ten thousand gold. The king offered two thousand for the party, and his [[High Marshal Toren Stannick|High Marshal]] made sure it sounded reasonable. His opinion of [[Cassian Vane]]: *"as annoying as he is, seems trustworthy."*
