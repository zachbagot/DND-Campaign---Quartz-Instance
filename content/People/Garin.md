Human fighter. Muscular, scarred, stoic, black buzz-cut hair. Fiercely loyal to [[Lyra Blackthorn]], though her recklessness grates on his discipline.

He was one of the three who ambushed the party in [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]]. He took a wolf bite to the neck in the fight, and [[Zaff]] spared his life in exchange for his iron sword.
