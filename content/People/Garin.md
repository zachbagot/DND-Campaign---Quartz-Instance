Human fighter. Muscular, scarred, stoic, black buzz-cut hair. Fiercely loyal to [[Lyra Blackthorn]], though her recklessness grates on his discipline.

He was one of the three who ambushed the party in [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]]. [[Zaff]] wild shaped into a wolf and bit him in the neck, then spared his life in exchange for his iron sword.

He was outside the bar with [[Lyra Blackthorn]] and [[Viola]] when [[Mayor Aldric Hawthorne]] offered the party a reward for his daughter, which is how the three of them knew where to wait.
