A goblin prince with a reputation for debauchery that had travelled a good deal further than he ever did.

He held a camp in the hills half a day's march from [[Briarwood]], with a gang of goblins around him. Close enough that the town knew roughly where he was, and nothing had ever been done about it.

When the red dragon dropped a goblin horde into [[Briarwood]] in [[Session 01 - The Burning of Briarwood]], those goblins took [[Mayor Aldric Hawthorne]]'s daughter [[Celeste Hawthorne]] and ran for those hills.

The party came for him in [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]]. They slaughtered every goblin in the cave and decapitated him at the end of it.

In [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]] [[Polly]] went back to the body, dismembered it, cut out the heart, and drove it into the wall of the cave.

**A faint shudder ran through the whole cave at that moment, and nobody has explained it.**
