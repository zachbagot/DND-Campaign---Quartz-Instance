The goblin prince who kidnapped [[Celeste Hawthorne]] in [[Session 01 - The Burning of Briarwood]].

The party found his hideout and decapitated him.

In [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]] [[Polly]] went back to the body, dismembered it, cut out the heart, and drove it into the wall of the cave. **A faint shudder ran through the whole cave at that moment, and nobody has explained it.**
