High Marshal Toren "Brass-Sight" Stannick, commander of the armies of [[Kharavel]]. A rock gnome with one eye and a monocle, and a man who says *indubitably*.

In [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]] he sat beside [[King Orrin Delabre]] and [[Prince Werrin Delabre]] while the party was offered two thousand gold for their part in the plan against [[Varkesh]]. While they considered it he cast a suggestion spell on the whole party, and every one of them found that two thousand gold sounded perfectly reasonable. [[Throkk]] has noted it: *"He's casting spells on me."*
