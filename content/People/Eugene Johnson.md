[[Throkk]]'s father. An old orc, a great drinker, found very drunk at [[Lord Forsythe]]'s bar in the shanty town on the lake shore in [[Session 07 - The Druid's Ruins]]. He fought at Blood Gulch Ridge, coming down off the ridge with the tribes, and thought his son had died there until Throkk walked into the bar.

He goes by Huge Johnson, and the title grows with retelling. Corporal Huge Johnson. Lieutenant Huge Johnson.

He toasted the party as the victors of Blood Gulch, challenged the room to a drinking game, and lost it to [[Polly]]. [[Enya]] charmed him so thoroughly he turned bright red and told her she was a little too young for him.

He knew the region's cities from memory: [[Caravelle]] to the north, [[Illyara]] to the east, and [[Varkesh]] between them to the northeast, the one place he begged his son not to go.

Throkk left him ten gold and a message for his brother Stanley, who has not been seen since the battle.
