The Thorn. He holds defence, justice and punishment for [[Ilyara]], and he prosecuted [[Polly]] in [[Session 08 - The Two Thrones]].

A satyr, broad and scarred, with enormous curled horns. His rangers have been dying in the forest for months and nobody has answered for it, so when a stranger opened a shopkeeper in the market in the middle of the day he wanted a death, and the gallery chanted with him.

> *"That's enough for me. Yep. Guilty. 100%. Death. Other council members?"*

Nothing anybody said in that room moved him. What moved him was watching [[Throkk]] catch a pair of animate roots in his bare hands and hold them, which he called martial power and respected on sight.

When the vote went against him he drew his own sword, carried it across the floor, and put it in [[Tallow]]'s hands so the wronged man could take his eye for an eye. Tallow dropped it.

He walked the party to the treeline himself, and what he said there was not a farewell.

> *"They should be honoring our borders. They should be staying out there while we get to stay here. Get to finding this. Otherwise I'll convince the council members that I'm right. And you need to die."*
