![[Wix.png]]

The sprite who raised [[Zaff]] after [[Rootfield]] fell. He was a child when the goblins destroyed his village, and Wix is who he grew up with instead.

She is [[Aster]]'s cousin, almost two hundred years old, and delighted by everything. By Aster's account she is "quite the character."

The party finally met her in [[Session 08 - The Two Thrones]], in a tree near the centre of the great flower. She fed them badly, admired their Seedfall seeds, and cheerfully dismantled the one thing [[Zaff]] had always assumed about her.

> *"I am a big part of the sprites, but I don't run the city, no. I think I just have a big impact in your life, so you made some assumptions."*

## What she said about Rootfield

Zaff told her he had come east to take revenge on [[Varkesh]]. She had been waiting a long time to have this conversation.

> *"I knew this day would come. I want you to know it wasn't just the goblins."*

Goblins were there, and that is where his eye went, but **somebody else was at Rootfield too**, and she does not know who. There is one way to find out and it is the way he was already going: go to the goblins. Not for vengeance.

> *"Don't let it destroy your soul!"*

She also warned him about the forest, which she has lived beside for two centuries.

> *"Zaff, the woods are alive, more than ever. And I don't know what's caused it. But now is not the time to make a fire."*

She named the five seats of [[The Council of Boroughs]] and marked where each of them lives on the party's map, minutes before the trial bells started ringing.
