An elf innkeeper, twenty-eight years old, who runs the [[Petal and Pitcher]] in [[Ilyara]]. She has magic of her own. No last name, like Enya, like Cher.

In [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]] she met the party at sunrise as they came in out of the forest and gave them all rooms. For free. She has five rooms, and one of them is about the size of a grain of pollen.
