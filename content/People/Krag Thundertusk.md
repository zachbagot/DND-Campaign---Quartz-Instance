Goliath, eight feet tall, lean rather than bulky. Ritual tattoos cover every inch of his grey skin. Completely hairless, with a surprisingly high-pitched voice.

He works as bodyguard to [[Beef the Bard]]. In [[Session 03 - Beef's Beefy Doors]] [[St. Joseph]] cast thaumaturgy to slam a door and it hit him, which broke the door and put the party in Beef's debt.
