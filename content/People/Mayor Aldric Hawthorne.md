![[Mayor Aldric Hawthorne.png]]

Mayor of [[Briarwood]] before the dragon came, and father of [[Celeste Hawthorne]].

A tall, heavy human in his mid forties, with speckled white and brown hair and skin gone thoroughly leathery from years out in the sun. A man who did not get the job by staying indoors.

Not to be confused with [[Captain Aldric Renn]], the human officer who commanded the caravan escort. Different man entirely, and the reason this wiki always uses full names.

## Session one

In [[Session 01 - The Burning of Briarwood]] he came out of the wreckage of his own town having just worked out that his daughter was gone. [[Celeste Hawthorne]] had been carried off by the goblins the dragon dropped, and somebody had seen which way they ran.

He begged the party to go after her and offered part of her dowry to bring her home.

He said it outside the bar, at normal volume, with [[Lyra Blackthorn]], [[Garin]] and [[Viola]] standing close enough to hear every word. That is why the party got ambushed on the way back.

## Session two

He met the party again once [[Celeste Hawthorne]] was safe, and negotiated the terms that sent them west: deliver her to [[Aquitaine]] for the full dowry, with a payment note, a carriage and horses.

[[Throkk]] talked him into sparing [[Lyra Blackthorn]] the stockades, which is the favour her brother [[Rylan]] later repaid in [[Aquitaine]].
