The heir of [[Kharavel]], son of [[King Orrin Delabre]].

In [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]] he argued for a full siege of [[Varkesh]], to starve the goblin city out and strip it of its resources, and to try an untested flying machine on it: *"This is the perfect test, daddy."* [[Polly]] raised an eyebrow at the daddy. The prince grinned back. The party sided with his father's feint.
