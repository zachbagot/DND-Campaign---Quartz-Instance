A vagrant in an alley in [[Aquitaine]] who spotted [[Throkk]] fleeing the Traveler's Trove robbery in [[Session 03 - Beef's Beefy Doors]].

[[Throkk]] picked him up, covered his mouth, and ran with him while the rest of the party laid a smokescreen behind them. He was released afterward with five copper and two goodberries.
