# Thrain Battleborn

A dwarf working a door in [[Aquitaine]] as a bouncer, which is a strange job for a level eight or nine oathbreaker paladin.

He grew up in the orphanage [[Polly]] helped on an earlier visit and now volunteers there. In [[Session 03 - Beef's Beefy Doors]] he confirmed the BB coin came from Beef's Beefy Doors.

He is covered in burns from a smelting accident, has a patchy beard, a milky blind eye, a mechanical leg brace, and a bitter expression over a kind heart.
