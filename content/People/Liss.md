![[Liss.png]]

One of [[Seraveth]]'s mages on the caravan. Late twenties, slight, androgynous.

She was decent to [[Polly]] on the march, and that relationship saved [[Polly]] from being caught breaking into her tent in [[Session 05 - The Caravan]]. [[Polly]] then tricked her into casting Comprehend Languages, which is how the party read the [[Death Rattles]].

She survived the rout at [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]] and was taken by the orcs.

The party traded [[Captain Aldric Renn]] to [[Vorrak Stonebell]] to get her released. She struck all five of them as a genuinely good person.

She ran, and she was free, and nobody knew where she went.

## Ilyara

She had run **east**, away from the desert and away from anyone likely to ask a mage where she had been while the orcs held her. She had been keeping her head down in [[Ilyara]] for a few days when she heard a gnome was on trial for his life.

She walked into the amphitheatre in [[Session 08 - The Two Thrones]] and testified for [[Polly]] in front of the whole city.

> *"They did get me out of some tough situations. I can definitely speak for their character. We don't have a lot of experience, but they did save my life. So I will absolutely say that there is good in them. All of them."*

Polly bowed to her so low his beard touched the ground.

**She travels with the party now.** She was in the forest for the blights and both wood rot trolls, put lightning down the line between them, and was one troll turn away from dying there. She is also the only member of the group who reacted to a god speaking out of the sky by backing slowly away from it.
