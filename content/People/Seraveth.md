# Seraveth

The mage who oversaw the orc caravan in [[Session 05 - The Caravan]]. Tall and narrow, pale, white-blonde, somewhat ambiguous in every direction.

Four mages answered to him on the march: Orvyn, [[Liss]], Cabel, and Mourne, plus three apprentices, Pip, Hara, and Voss.

He is not a soldier. The soldiers belonged to [[Captain Aldric Renn]]. Seraveth was there in some other capacity, and what the mages were writing in their journals is the reason [[Polly]] broke into two tents. See [[Death Rattles]].

**He was run down to a single hit point at [[Session 06 - Blood Gulch Ridge]].**

[[Enya]] cut him off from his own mages with a wall of fog, so that none of the casters whose job was to protect him could see or reach him. Then she put an arcane orb into him, and [[St. Joseph]]'s spiritual weapon reached him as well. He got a teleport off and vanished before they could finish him, but [[Polly]] took his arm off first and picked it up.

The party still has the arm. He is still out there, one arm lighter, and he knows exactly who did it.
