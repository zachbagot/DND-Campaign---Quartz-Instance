*Maria in Ilyara, keeping close to St. Joseph.*

A little girl of [[Ilyara]] and the smallest of [[St. Joseph]]'s disciples.

In [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]] she was one of the ten children St. Joseph sent to pick the pockets of the town, and she picked the wrong one. She came back with her face cut open. St. Joseph healed her with his own magic, and after that she did not leave his side. He named her Maria. She went through the teleportation circle to [[Kharavel]] with [[Reddy Riz]] and two others.
