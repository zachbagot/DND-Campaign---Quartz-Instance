Childhood friend of [[Celeste Hawthorne]], and the daughter of [[Maris]] the alchemist of [[Briarwood]].

Quietly striking. Pale, moonlit skin and long obsidian hair with silver undertones. Deep blue eyes flecked with something like stars. She carries both innocence and an old sadness.

She travelled with the party from [[Briarwood]] to [[Aquitaine]] in [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]], as company for [[Celeste Hawthorne]] on the way to a marriage.

**When the party delivered the two women, both [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]] and [[Prince Caelen Tidelorn]] looked at them and assumed Seraphine was the bride.**

She remains in [[Aquitaine]] with [[Celeste Hawthorne]].
