The goblin who destroyed [[Rootfield]].

Thirteen or fourteen years ago Xeraf led the pack that killed a town of roughly a hundred people gathering food for the winter. They used the eight-year-old [[Zaff]] as bait to draw the defenders out, and when it was over they left him alive in the ruins without food or water. That is where his eye went, and it is the reason he came east at all.

The party met him at last in [[Session 08 - The Two Thrones]], in the clearing of the two thrones, wearing military dress and commanding soldiers. He described himself as one of the leaders of Varkesh's military.

**He did not recognise Zaff.** Not denial. No recognition at all.

> *"You don't remember the town?"* — *"I've been to many towns."*
> *"The town you've burned?"* — *"I've burned many towns."*

Zaff lifted his eyepatch and showed him what was underneath.

> *"Look, I've seen many missing eyes. I'm sorry you're missing your eye. I don't know you though."*

Pressed, he called it self-defence and retribution for months of raiders, and said that now was not the time because this is a holy place. He referred to his own prince as beneath him. Then he laughed, called Zaff a crazy man, and walked away with his soldiers.

Zaff caught his shoulder as he went.

> *"This will be the last time this is a peaceful attempt at communication."*
