Ruler of [[Aquitaine]]. Composed and charismatic, with sea-blue eyes and long silver-streaked hair like cresting waves.

Known for fairness and diplomacy, and for an almost intuitive bond with the old water-magic of his city. Beloved by his people. He carries a private worry about enemies that he does not discuss.

He received [[Celeste Hawthorne]] and [[Seraphine]] from the party in [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]], and mistook the second for the first.

He did not have the dowry on hand the next morning, which is how the party ended up with a day free in [[Aquitaine]] and a thieves guild coin in their pocket. After [[Polly]] groped [[Son Soleth]] at his court, he assigned them to escort the caravan as punishment. See [[Session 04 - The Iron Net]].
