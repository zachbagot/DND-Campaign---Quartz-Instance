A sprite scout of [[The Council of Boroughs]], pink-winged, married to [[Aster]]. She watched the party with him from the forest's edge in [[Session 07 - The Druid's Ruins]].

She ran the party's sprite dust flight like a flight attendant: *"Now that we're gonna be your flight companions tonight, we better really buckle up! Everyone grab each other's hands, we're gonna make a chain!"* The chain worked until [[Polly]] let go of it to smoke, at which point four passengers proved too much for one sprite and everyone came down at [[Rootfield]].

When not on scout duty, she and Aster paint flowers.

In [[Session 08 - The Two Thrones]] she and [[Aster]] testified at [[Polly]]'s trial, and their account of flying the party in that morning was what cleared him of the four deaths in the forest.
