# Viola

Tiefling wizard. Red-skinned, golden-eyed, ambitious, with small curling horns and a dangerous curiosity. Brilliant but impulsive, and her fire magic flares with her temper.

She rode with [[Lyra Blackthorn]] and [[Garin]]. In the ambush in [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]] she lost an eye to [[Polly]].

**She escaped.** [[Garin]] was spared and [[Lyra Blackthorn]] was taken captive, but Viola got away clean and nobody has seen her since. She is out there somewhere, down one eye, with a reason to remember the party.

She was outside the bar with [[Lyra Blackthorn]] and [[Garin]] when [[Mayor Aldric Hawthorne]] offered the party a reward for his daughter, which is how the three of them knew where to wait.
