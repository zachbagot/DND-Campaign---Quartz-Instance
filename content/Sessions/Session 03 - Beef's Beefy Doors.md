The party comes to collect a dowry, gets pulled into [[Aquitaine]]'s criminal underworld instead, and robs a shop for a tiefling who sells doors.

## The Duke has no money today

The party reported back to [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]] to collect payment for delivering [[Celeste Hawthorne]]. He did not have the coin on hand, and told them to come back in the morning.

That left them a day in [[Aquitaine]] with nothing to do.

## Following the coin

The **BB** coin that had been planted on [[Zaff]] on the road turned out to be a thieves guild token. Shown to the right person, it got them directions.

Along the way [[Polly]] stopped at the orphanage he had helped on a previous visit. [[Thrain Battleborn]], an adult volunteer who grew up there, confirmed the coin came from **Beef's Beefy Doors**.

## The racket

[[Beef the Bard]] is a tiefling woman, and she runs [[Aquitaine]]. The city has a Duke, but the thieves guild answers to her.

Beef's Beefy Doors is a front, and the racket is elegant. Her people break your door. Then they sell you protection so it does not happen again. Then they sell you the replacement door. You pay twice and thank them for it.

[[Rylan]], older brother of [[Lyra Blackthorn]], put the party's name forward. The party had spared his sister, so the guild owed them a hearing.

Then [[St. Joseph]] slammed one of Beef's doors into a bodyguard and broke it.

Beef was outraged. The party now owed her a favour.

## Traveler's Trove

The favour was a job. Break down the doors of Alden Merrik's **Traveler's Trove General Shop**.

The party scouted it first. [[Polly]] picked the back lock. [[Zaff]] identified a trap as a snack, and [[Throkk]] ate it and was poisoned. [[Polly]] found a potion that cured him.

They came away with a haul, though the job was Beef's and the goods were hers:

- Ever-Dry Socks, magical
- An amulet of Feather Fall, single use
- A healer's kit
- A potion of healing and a potion of greater healing
- A suit of plate armour
- An ornamental ruby-encrusted axe
- Iron-studded leather clothing
- An adventurer's journal

They broke the shop's back door on the way out, as instructed.

The ornamental axe, the iron-studded leather and the plate armour went back to [[Beef the Bard]]. She took the pieces worth reselling. The party kept the socks, the amulet, the healer's kit, the potions and the journal, which is the difference between a crew on a job and a band of adventurers who struck it rich.

[[Throkk]] was spotted by a vagrant in the alley. He picked the man up, covered his mouth, and ran. The vagrant, [[Tharin]], was later let go with five copper and two goodberries.

The party threw a smokescreen into the alley and escaped back toward Beef's.

## Notable in the city

- [[Krag Thundertusk]], an eight-foot goliath, works as Beef's bodyguard.
- [[Thrain Battleborn]] is a dwarf bouncer and a level eight or nine oathbreaker paladin, which is a strange thing to find working a door.
- A small orphan named Dominic got a copper coin from [[Polly]].
