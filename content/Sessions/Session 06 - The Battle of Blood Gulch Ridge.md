Played **11 July 2026**, the second half of a battle too big to finish in one night. It began on 27 June in [[Session 05 - The Caravan]] and is told here in one piece.

Five people who had spent a week walking beside a column of prisoners stopped being escorts, and by the end of the night they were tribe.

## Where it started

A week into the march, the caravan reached the river. [[Kael Sorra]] had already told them what was coming: an ambush at the ford, orcs on the ridge above the crossing. The party was at the back of the column, close enough to reach the fighting and far enough to pretend they had not seen it.

Then the warhorn went, and the sky went dark with arrows falling on the vanguard. The guards locked into a phalanx and started up the slope, chulls screaming in their harnesses, orc civilians scattering between two armies with nowhere to go.

## The choice

The party were lawful escorts on a Duke's assignment. Siding with the humans was legal, safe, and paid. Siding with the orcs meant turning on soldiers they had shared a road with, in the open, in front of witnesses, with no way back.

They picked the orcs. All five, without much argument. Everything since has come from that.

## The battle

It was too big to fight on a grid, so it was fought as two armies whose strength and nerve could each break, and **the orcs never lost the contest. Not once, across the whole engagement.** The guards did the correct thing against arrows from a ridge: shields locked, grinding up the slope. It is not the correct thing against five people already standing behind you.

[[Throkk]] did not do subtle. He went in headfirst against a squad of human heavy infantry, the formation closed, and they beat him half to pieces. [[St. Joseph]] got him back on his feet inside it, then set a **spiritual weapon** loose in the human ranks, a blade with nobody holding it, carving through the guards for the rest of the battle.

The party panicked a team of draft oxen through the guards' backup lines and an entire squad of archers went under the hooves. An orc sorcerer on the ridge worked out what was happening below and started throwing fire **for** them. Then [[Kael Sorra]] came off the ridge into the melee, pressed a pouch into a hand, and said the line the table has not forgotten:

> *"Vorrak is watching from the ridge, make your strikes look good."*

Elder Bog Ashveil rode his chull alongside them mid-battle to offer a share of the caravan's hidden treasury for the knight on the left flank, and orc wolf cavalry leapt down to flank through the guards. The party were not alone inside a human column any more. They were the hinge of an army.

[[Son Soleth]] was on that field. The dignitary [[Polly]] had groped at the Duke's court, whose demand for punishment is the only reason the party was ever sent to the plains. [[Zaff]] caught him and slowed him. [[Enya]] killed him. The man whose wounded pride wrote their orders died under those orders.

With his column coming apart, [[Seraveth]] stopped holding back and unleashed a golem into the middle of it. So [[Enya]] cut him off instead: **a wall of fog between Seraveth and his mages**, leaving the man whose casters keep him alive alone in the open.

Then the stroke that ended it. The human mages had their full attention up the hill, working against the orc archers, and the party was already behind them. A line of mages with their backs turned. They destroyed the caravan's entire magical defence in one blow, and **that is what broke the human line.**

They went after [[Seraveth]] himself. [[Enya]] put an arcane orb into him through her own fog and [[St. Joseph]]'s unheld blade found him too. At a single hit point, alone, he got a teleport off. He did not get all of himself out. [[Polly]] took the arm off and picked it up. **The party still has it.**

Worn down and still lethal, [[Captain Aldric Renn]] finally saw what had opened his line from the inside, and came for the party personally with the battle already lost behind him.

## How it ended

Not with the humans wiped out. With the humans breaking. The line lost its nerve before it lost its numbers, the retreat turned into a rout, and the rout turned into a slaughter of men who were already running. Some got away. [[Liss]] was one of them, briefly.

## What the field gave up

Five things came off the dead when the fighting stopped, and every one carries a question nobody has answered.

- **[[Ashveil's Oathbreaker]]**, to [[Throkk]]. A greataxe pried off an orc champion who had been pressed into the ambushers' service. His clan-marks were filed off the blade and the job was left unfinished, so the grooves still hold the shape of the name **Ashveil**.
- **[[Cloak of the Fading Step]]**, to [[Polly]]. Off an assassin. Elven weave, human dye, mage-work enchantment, three factions in one garment, and it fits Polly as though it were cut for a gnome.
- **[[Blessed Dawnwater]]**, to [[St. Joseph]]. A healing potion brewed for medics who work at night. It turns the drinker into a lamp for an hour.
- **[[Ring of the Blooming Vine]]**, to [[Zaff]]. Off a human field-medic who died dragging an orc child to cover. It blooms one white flower every dawn.
- **[[Seraveth's Lost Sceptre]]**, to [[Enya]]. Found under a dead war-mage whose hands were burned where he had gripped it, and the make matches [[Seraveth]]'s own work. In Enya's hand it did not burn. It settled.

## The trade

They took [[Captain Aldric Renn]] alive, and a captured human officer is worth a great deal to an orc resistance. [[Vorrak Stonebell]] wanted him. The party wanted [[Liss]], who had been decent to [[Polly]] on the march for no reason that served her, and who had covered for him when he broke into her tent. Now she was in orc hands with a war just finished around her.

So they traded the captain for the mage. Liss walked out free, and she ran. Renn is a prisoner in the shanty settlement on the lake shore.

## The invitation

Afterward [[Vorrak Stonebell]] came to them himself.

> *"I'm not going to thank you. Words are cheap. Three days east there's a marsh no map shows. Under it is Ghol Uzhak, the old den. There's a fire there older than the worlds, and a woman who speaks for it. If it sees in you what I saw today, it will mark you, and that mark makes you tribe. Not guests. Tribe. No human has stood inside Ghol Uzhak since before your people had names. We walk at dawn. Eat something first, I'm not presenting you to my grandmother's fire looking like drowned cats."*

## Ghol Uzhak

Three days east, under a marsh on no map, there is a lake. You walk into the water, and then you are looking up at it. [[Ghol Uzhak]] sits between planes, the ceiling of the world is the underside of the lake, and the village stands in sunlight that shifts like water. At its centre is a single hollow tusk two hundred feet across, left by some beast from before the current peoples, and people live inside it.

Before any ceremony came the children's welcome: run the scaffolding to the top of the tusk, jump, touch the underside of the water that is the sky, and fall into the netting. All five did it, each with their own flourish, in front of a village that had never had an outsider in it. They got applause.

## The branding

Then the fire at the base of the tusk, and [[Yagra Emberveil]], ancient, blind-eyed, tracking each of them precisely anyway. She drew a coal from the fire with her bare hand. On skin it brings no pain, only cold and the smell of rain on hot stone, and the mark rises from underneath.

Five marks came up, and she reacted differently to every one. [[Throkk]] took the Broken Tusk Made Whole, a mark that exists in no tradition anyone present had heard of. [[Polly]] took the Key of Smoke, a mark of doors, and got the only pat on the hand she gave all night. [[Zaff]] took the Seed in the Ash. *"The sky sent us a gardener. Good. The sky owes us."* [[St. Joseph]] took the Hearth With Two Shadows, two lights competing to say what he is. [[Enya]] took the Star Held in a Fist, and Yagra looked past the mark straight into her eyes: *"Hold tight, little fire."* The marks and her words are kept in full in [[The Five Brands]].

When all five were marked, the crankiness went out of her voice once:

> *"Any hearth of the people is your hearth. Any orc who sees this mark owes you shade, water, and truth, and you owe the same. You did not join the tribe tonight. The tribe is not a thing you join. The fire simply stopped pretending you were strangers."*

She also said the fire had been expecting them, and she did not say what for.

## The party after

Then Ghol Uzhak threw a party for the five people it had just stopped pretending were strangers. They ate with the orcs and drank with them, and it went the way that goes. Everyone got thoroughly drunk. [[Enya]] flirted with a random orc. They slept it off inside a village no outsider had slept in for ten thousand years.

That is where the session ended: they traded and stocked up on the way out, armour for [[Enya]], [[St. Joseph]] and [[Throkk]], fourteen grams of ashroot from Elder Senne Gorefang for [[Polly]], and [[Zaff]] quietly growing his own supply. The party stands at the lake, freshly branded, well fed, badly hungover, wanted by a human duchy they betrayed, carrying a mage's severed arm and no particular plan.
