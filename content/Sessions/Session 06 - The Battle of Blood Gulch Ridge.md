Played **11 July 2026**, the second half of a battle too big to finish in one night. It began on 27 June in [[Session 05 - The Caravan]] and is told here in one piece.

Five people who had spent a week walking beside a column of prisoners stopped being escorts, and by the end of the night they were tribe.

## The choice

A week into the march the caravan reached the river, and [[Kael Sorra]] had already named what was waiting: an ambush at the ford, orcs on the ridge above the crossing. Then the warhorn went and the sky went dark with arrows. The guards locked into a phalanx and started up the slope, chulls screaming in their harnesses, orc civilians scattering between two armies with nowhere to go.

The party were lawful escorts on a Duke's assignment. Siding with the humans was legal, safe, and paid. Siding with the orcs meant turning on soldiers they had shared a road with, in the open, in front of witnesses, with no way back.

They picked the orcs. All five, without much argument. Everything since has come from that.

## The battle

It was too big for a grid, so it was fought as two armies whose strength and nerve could each break, and **the orcs never lost the contest. Not once.** The guards did the correct thing against arrows from a ridge: shields locked, grinding uphill. It is not the correct thing against five people already standing behind you.

[[Throkk]] did not do subtle. He went in headfirst against a squad of human heavy infantry, the formation closed, and they beat him half to pieces. [[St. Joseph]] got him back on his feet inside it, then set a **spiritual weapon** loose in the ranks, a blade with nobody holding it, carving through the guards for the rest of the fight.

The party panicked a team of draft oxen through the backup lines and a squad of archers went under the hooves. An orc sorcerer on the ridge worked out what was happening below and started throwing fire **for** them. Then [[Kael Sorra]] came off the ridge into the melee, pressed a pouch into a hand, and said the line the table has not forgotten:

> *"Vorrak is watching from the ridge, make your strikes look good."*

[[Son Soleth]] was on that field, the dignitary [[Polly]] had groped at the Duke's court, whose demand for punishment is the only reason the party was sent to the plains at all. [[Zaff]] caught him and slowed him. [[Enya]] killed him.

With his column coming apart, [[Seraveth]] unleashed a golem into the middle of it. [[Enya]] answered by cutting him off from everyone who kept him standing, **a wall of fog between the Mage Warden and his own mages**.

Then the stroke that ended it. The human mages had their attention up the hill, working against the orc archers, and the party was already behind them. A line of mages with their backs turned. They destroyed the caravan's magical defence in one blow, and the line broke. Not wiped out, just unwilling, the retreat turning into a rout and the rout into a slaughter of men already running.

They ran [[Seraveth]] down inside Enya's fog, an arcane orb and an unheld blade, until he got a teleport off at a single hit point. He did not get all of himself out. [[Polly]] took the arm off and picked it up. **The party still has it.**

## What the field gave up

Five things came off the dead when the fighting stopped, and every one carries a question.

- **[[Ashveil's Oathbreaker]]**, to [[Throkk]]. Pried off an orc champion pressed into the ambushers' service, his clan-marks filed off the blade and the job left unfinished, so the grooves still hold the shape of the name **Ashveil**.
- **[[Cloak of the Fading Step]]**, to [[Polly]]. Off an assassin. Elven weave, human dye, mage-work enchantment, three factions in one garment, and it fits Polly as though it were cut for a gnome.
- **[[Blessed Dawnwater]]**, to [[St. Joseph]]. Brewed for medics who work at night. It turns the drinker into a lamp for an hour.
- **[[Ring of the Blooming Vine]]**, to [[Zaff]]. Off a human field-medic who died dragging an orc child to cover. It blooms one white flower every dawn.
- **[[Seraveth's Lost Sceptre]]**, to [[Enya]]. Found under a war-mage whose hands were burned where he had gripped it, and the make matches [[Seraveth]]'s own. In Enya's hand it did not burn. It settled.

## The trade

They took [[Captain Aldric Renn]] alive, and a captured human officer is worth a great deal to an orc resistance. [[Vorrak Stonebell]] wanted him. The party wanted [[Liss]], who had been decent to [[Polly]] on the march for no reason that served her, had covered for him when he broke into her tent, and was now in orc hands with a war just finished around her.

So they traded the captain for the mage. Liss walked out free, and she ran. Renn is a prisoner in the shanty settlement on the lake shore.

## The invitation

Then [[Vorrak Stonebell]] came to them himself.

> *"I'm not going to thank you. Words are cheap. Three days east there's a marsh no map shows. Under it is Ghol Uzhak, the old den. There's a fire there older than the worlds, and a woman who speaks for it. If it sees in you what I saw today, it will mark you, and that mark makes you tribe. Not guests. Tribe. We walk at dawn."*

## Ghol Uzhak

Three days east, under a marsh on no map, there is a lake. You walk into the water, and then you are looking up at it. [[Ghol Uzhak]] sits between planes, the ceiling of the world is the underside of the lake, and the village stands in sunlight that shifts like water. At its centre is a hollow tusk two hundred feet across, left by some beast from before the current peoples, and people live inside it. No outsider had stood there in ten thousand years.

Before any ceremony came the children's welcome: run the scaffolding to the top of the tusk, jump, touch the underside of the water that is the sky, and drop into the netting. All five did it, each with their own flourish. They got applause.

## The branding

Then the fire at the base of the tusk, and [[Yagra Emberveil]], ancient, blind-eyed, tracking each of them precisely anyway. She drew a coal out with her bare hand. On skin it brings no pain, only cold and the smell of rain on hot stone, and the mark rises from underneath.

She reacted differently to every one. [[Throkk]] took the Broken Tusk Made Whole, which exists in no tradition anyone present had heard of. [[Polly]] took the Key of Smoke, a mark of doors, and got the only pat on the hand she gave all night. [[Zaff]] took the Seed in the Ash: *"The sky sent us a gardener. Good. The sky owes us."* [[St. Joseph]] took the Hearth With Two Shadows. [[Enya]] took the Star Held in a Fist, and Yagra looked past the mark straight into her eyes: *"Hold tight, little fire."* All five are kept in full in [[The Five Brands]].

Then the crankiness went out of her voice, once:

> *"Any hearth of the people is your hearth. Any orc who sees this mark owes you shade, water, and truth, and you owe the same. You did not join the tribe tonight. The tribe is not a thing you join. The fire simply stopped pretending you were strangers."*

She also said the fire had been expecting them, and she did not say what for.

## After

Then the village threw a party. They ate and drank with the orcs and it went the way that goes: everyone thoroughly drunk, [[Enya]] flirting with a random orc, all of them sleeping it off where no outsider had slept in ten thousand years. On the way out they stocked up, armour for three of them and fourteen grams of ashroot from Elder Senne Gorefang for [[Polly]].

The party stands at the lake, freshly branded, well fed, badly hungover, wanted by a human duchy they betrayed, carrying a mage's severed arm and no particular plan.
