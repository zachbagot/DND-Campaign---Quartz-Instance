Played 27 June. The session the campaign turns on. Five people who had spent a week walking beside a column of prisoners decided they were not escorts any more, and by the end of the night they were tribe.

## Where it started

A week into the march, the caravan reached the river.

[[Kael Sorra]] had already told them what was coming. An ambush at the ford, orcs on the ridge above the crossing, and a choice that could not be dodged by standing still. The party was at the back of the column, three rounds from the ridgeline, close enough to reach the fighting and far enough to pretend they had not seen it.

Then the warhorn went, and the sky went dark with arrows.

Hundreds of them, falling on the vanguard. The guards locked into a phalanx and started up the slope. Chulls screamed in their harnesses. Orc civilians, the ones who had been walking and dying in that column for a week, scattered between two armies with nowhere to go.

## The choice

This is the thing worth remembering about Blood Gulch Ridge, more than any individual moment in it.

The party were lawful escorts on a sanctioned assignment given to them by a Duke. Siding with the humans was the legal choice, the safe choice, and the one that got them paid. Siding with the orcs meant turning on the soldiers they had shared a road with, in the open, in front of witnesses, with no way back afterward.

They picked the orcs. All five of them, without much argument about it.

Everything since has come from that.

## The battle

It was too big to fight on a grid, so it was fought as two armies with numbers that could break. Each side carried its strength and its will as separate things, and either one hitting bottom would end them. Lose the bodies and the fighting force is gone. Lose the nerve and the whole line comes apart and leaves its commanders standing alone.

**The orcs never lost the contest.** Not once, across the whole engagement. The dice were absurd and the party spent the entire battle pressing an advantage that only widened.

They fought it from inside the caravan, which is the part the human command never accounted for. The guards were watching the ridge. The people wrecking them were already behind the line.

Somewhere in the middle of it a team of draft oxen was driven into the guards' backup lines, right where Elder Mara Duskstone was trying to get the orc civilians moving, and an entire squad of human archers went under the hooves.

An orc sorcerer on the ridge worked out what was happening below and started throwing power down onto the party rather than at them.

Then [[Kael Sorra]] came off the ridge and into the melee beside them, pressed something into a hand, and said the line the table has not forgotten:

> *"Vorrak is watching from the ridge, make your strikes look good."*

Elder Bog Ashveil rode his chull up alongside and rasped an order to put down a human knight holding the left flank, with a share of the caravan's hidden treasury for whoever managed it.

And near the end, [[Captain Aldric Renn]] finally understood exactly who had opened his line from the inside. He pulled a squad of Heavy Infantry out of formation, took Sergeant Dava with him, and came for the party personally with everything he had left.

## What they did

**[[Enya]] put a cloud of fog across the field.** With a human army closing on them in the open, she took away the one thing that army had, which was sight. Everything the party managed after that happened inside her fog. For someone who had joined the campaign a session earlier, it was the call that decided the fight.

**[[Zaff]] slowed [[Son Soleth]].** The dignitary from [[Dur Soleth]], the same man [[Polly]] had groped at the Duke's court and whose outrage put the party on this road in the first place, was on the field. Zaff caught him and took his speed away.

**[[Enya]] killed him.** Slowed, in the fog, with nowhere to go. The man whose wounded pride sent them to the plains died on the plains, killed by the newest member of the party.

**[[Polly]] took [[Seraveth]]'s arm.** Working inside the fog the party ran the mage overseeing the entire march down to a single hit point. He got a teleport off and vanished before they could finish it, but he did not leave whole. Polly cut the arm off and picked it up off the ground.

The party still has it.

## How it ended

**Not with the humans wiped out. With the humans breaking.**

The line lost its nerve before it lost its numbers, and the moment it did, the retreat turned into a rout and the rout turned into a slaughter of men who were already running. Some got away. [[Liss]] was one of them.

[[Seraveth]] teleported out at one hit point, one arm lighter.

## The trade

They took [[Captain Aldric Renn]] alive.

A captured human officer is worth a great deal to an orc resistance, and [[Vorrak Stonebell]] wanted him. The party had something they wanted more.

[[Liss]] had been decent to [[Polly]] on the march. Not strategically decent, not decent because it served her, just a person being kind in a column where kindness cost something. When the party broke into her tent she covered for them. The whole table read her as genuinely good, and now she was in orc hands with a war just finished around her.

So they gave [[Vorrak Stonebell]] the captain and asked for the mage.

He took the trade. [[Liss]] walked out free, and she ran, and nobody has heard from her since.

[[Captain Aldric Renn]] is a prisoner now, held in the shanty settlement on the shore of the lake, outside the pocket dimension.

## The invitation

Afterward [[Vorrak Stonebell]] came to them himself, and what he offered had not been offered to a human in ten thousand years.

> *"I'm not going to thank you. Words are cheap.*
>
> *Three days east there's a marsh no map shows. Under it is Ghol Uzhak, the old den. The root of every tribe that's ever carried a banner. There's a fire there older than the worlds, and a woman who speaks for it. If it sees in you what I saw today, it will mark you, and that mark makes you tribe. Not guests. Tribe.*
>
> *No human has stood inside Ghol Uzhak since before your people had names. The elders will argue. Let them. You bled in the water with us.*
>
> *We walk at dawn. Eat something first, I'm not presenting you to my grandmother's fire looking like drowned cats."*

## Ghol Uzhak

Three days east, under a marsh that appears on no map, there is a lake.

You walk into the water. Then you are looking up at it.

[[Ghol Uzhak]] sits between spatial planes. The ceiling of the world is the underside of the lake, and the sun still comes through it, so the whole village stands in daylight filtered through moving water, and the light shifts across the ground all day.

At the centre of it is a tusk.

One tusk, two hundred feet across and a hundred and fifty tall, thirty feet through at the base, curving up out of the ground. It is hollow, and people live inside it. Rough scaffolding climbs the outside like a set of stairs, and netting is strung underneath, between the tusk and the ground and the water overhead.

Nobody knows what it came from. What the orcs hold is that there were enormous beasts in the world before the current peoples, and that they died out as cleverer things prospered, and this is what one of them left.

### The game

Before any ceremony, the party got invited into what the children of Ghol Uzhak do for fun.

You run the scaffolding to the top. You jump. You reach up and touch the underside of the water that is the sky. Then you fall, and the netting catches you.

All five of them did it. Each of them did it their own way, with their own flourish, in front of a village of orcs who had never had a human standing in it.

They got applause.

That is the moment worth holding onto from this session. Not the battle. Five outsiders showing off for a crowd that three days earlier had every reason to kill them.

## The branding

Then the fire, at the base of the tusk.

[[Yagra Emberveil]] was already talking when they came in, mid-sentence, as though the conversation had started before they existed. Ancient, small for an orc, bent like a root, both eyes clouded white, and tracking each of them precisely anyway.

She drew a coal out of the fire with her bare hand. Her skin did not redden.

> *"I do not choose the mark. I have never chosen a mark. I am the hand. The fire is the eye. If you do not like what it sees, it does not care. It has seen everything burn at least once. It is difficult to impress."*

The coal touches skin and there is no pain. There is cold, and the smell of rain on hot stone. The mark comes up from underneath, like something rising through water, and it takes about three breaths.

She did not know what was coming either. She watched each one arrive with real curiosity, and she reacted differently to every single one. Full detail in [[The Five Brands]].

**[[Throkk]] took the Broken Tusk Made Whole.** A tusk snapped in two, the halves bound by a ring of flame. Among orcs a broken tusk is the mark of shame and exile. A mended one exists in no tradition anyone present had ever heard of. [[Yagra Emberveil]] went still for a long moment and said, quietly, not to him: *"So. It does forgive."* She would not explain. Old orcs may touch that mark without asking and walk away without saying anything.

**[[Polly]] took the Key of Smoke.** A skeleton key rendered in curling smoke, the teeth dissolving at the edges as though it were halfway to vanishing. Not a mark of thievery. A mark of doors. Yagra snorted at it. *"Of course. Keep it away from my things."* Then she patted Polly's hand once, which she did for nobody else all night.

**[[Zaff]] took the Seed in the Ash.** A seed cracking open, its first root going down into a bed of ash. Aasimar are creatures of the sky, of light arriving from above, and the fire marked him with something that grows up out of burned ground instead. Yagra nodded like a suspicion had been confirmed. *"The sky sent us a gardener. Good. The sky owes us."*

**[[St. Joseph]] took the Hearth With Two Shadows.** A hearthfire throwing two shadows in opposite directions. A priest carries his god with him, so he never stands anywhere alone, but two shadows also means two lights competing to say what he is. She studied that one longer than the others. *"Two lights. Hm. When they argue, priest, and they will argue, stand still. Let them fight over you. Whichever one stays is yours."*

**[[Enya]] took the Star Held in a Fist.** A four-pointed star gripped tight in a closed hand, light leaking between the fingers. Sorcery is power born in the blood rather than studied or granted, and the fire marked the holding of it rather than the power itself. Yagra did something here she had not done all ceremony. She looked away from the mark and directly into Enya's eyes, for an uncomfortably long beat. Then: *"Hold tight, little fire."* Nothing else.

When all five were marked she spoke to them together, and the crankiness went out of her voice for the first time.

> *"Hear what you now are. Any hearth of the people is your hearth. Any orc who sees this mark owes you shade, water, and truth, and you owe the same, so do not make me regret you. You did not join the tribe tonight. Understand this. The tribe is not a thing you join. The fire simply stopped pretending you were strangers."*

Then, immediately cranky again, flapping a hand at them to get out:

> *"Now go away. Eat something. Vorrak, they fought in a river all day, why do they look hungry, what is wrong with you."*

She had also said, earlier, that the fire had been expecting them.

**She never said what for.**

## Before they left

They traded and stocked up on the way out.

[[Enya]] bought petite light leather armour for nine gold. [[St. Joseph]] paid ten for his, [[Throkk]] paid five for his.

Elder Senne Gorefang gave [[Polly]] fourteen grams of ashroot. The seven he had been carrying had already gone into building a hallucinogenic bomb, so he is carrying fourteen now. [[Zaff]] grew a hundred and thirteen grams of devil's lettuce, using magic to speed it along. Ashroot is the serious hallucinogen of the two. Devil's lettuce is mild.

## Where they stand

At the lake. Freshly branded. Marked as tribe by a fire older than the world, wanted by a human duchy they betrayed, carrying a mage's severed arm and no particular plan.
