# Session 06: Blood Gulch Ridge

Played 27 June. The party picks a side, wins a battle, trades a prisoner, and walks into a place no human has entered in ten thousand years.

## The battle

The engagement was too large for a grid, so it ran on faction trackers. Both the Human Caravan and the Ridge Orcs carried **10 HP**, for numbers and supply, and **10 Morale**, for discipline and will.

At the top of each round a player rolled a straight d20 for their faction against the DM's roll for the other side. The lower roll cost that side a point, and the losing side chose whether to spend HP or Morale. Then a roll on the Dynamic Battlefield Table set the round. Zero Morale meant the rank and file shattered and left their leaders exposed. Zero HP meant the fighting force was gone.

**The party sided with the orcs and turned on the caravan from inside it.**

| Roll | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1 | [[Polly]] gets into the mage tents and comes out with the journal and Mourne's personal effects |
| 2 | Not recorded |
| 3 | The party panics draft oxen into the guards' backup lines near Elder Mara Duskstone and the orc civilians. A squad of human archers is trampled to nothing |
| 4 | An orc sorcerer on the ridge sees the betrayal and throws support down onto the party |
| 5 | [[Kael Sorra]] slips them a pouch and joins the melee. *"Vorrak is watching from the ridge, make your strikes look good."* Orcs gain a Morale |
| 6 | Elder Bog Ashveil rides up on his chull and orders a hit on a human knight holding the left flank |
| 7 | [[Captain Aldric Renn]] sees the betrayal directly and pulls Heavy Infantry out of formation, with Sergeant Dava, to kill the party specifically |

The party rolled well and never gave up the advantage. **The battle was decided on Morale, not HP.** The human line broke before it was destroyed and the rout became a slaughter of soldiers who were already running. Some survived. [[Liss]] was among them.

[[Seraveth]] escaped at one hit point. He left his arm behind. The party has it.

## The prisoner trade

[[Captain Aldric Renn]] was taken alive.

The party traded him to the orcs for [[Liss]], who had been decent to [[Polly]] on the march and struck the whole party as a genuinely good person. The orcs took the better prize. [[Liss]] walked away free and is still out there somewhere.

[[Captain Aldric Renn]] is now held in the shanty settlement on the lake shore, outside the pocket dimension.

## Into Ghol Uzhak

[[Vorrak Stonebell]] extended the invitation himself. The party walked into the lake and came out looking up at water. Full description in [[Ghol Uzhak]].

Before the ceremony they were invited into the local children's game. The scaffolding runs up the side of the great tusk like rough stairs, and the trick is to climb, jump, touch the underside of the water that forms the sky, and drop back down into the netting.

All five did it, each with their own flourish, and each of them got applause from the orcs watching.

## The branding

[[Yagra Emberveil]] performed it at the fire below the tusk. She does not choose the marks. She says she is the hand and the fire is the eye.

Each of the five received a mark of their own. They are recorded in [[The Five Brands]].

> *"Any hearth of the people is your hearth. Any orc who sees this mark owes you shade, water, and truth, and you owe the same. You did not join the tribe tonight. The tribe is not a thing you join. The fire simply stopped pretending you were strangers."*

She also told them the fire had been expecting them. She did not say what for.

## Where the party is now

Standing at the lake, freshly branded, ready to travel.
