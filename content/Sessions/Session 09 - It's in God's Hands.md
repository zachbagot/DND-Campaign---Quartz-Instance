The party walks out of the forest as heroes and spends one day in [[Ilyara]] paying what they owe. By the next midnight they have been hired to put a goblin prince on his father's throne, and [[St. Joseph]] has four children who will not go home.

## Morning in the clearing

The vines peeled off [[Zaff]] and left him jacked, like something strong had been pumped into his veins. He thought *I am so ready to kill some goblins*, and had the sense not to say it to fifty of them. Being named a prophet lifted [[St. Joseph]] from the fifth level of exhaustion to as spry as a newborn, and Zaff found he could pass what was running through him into [[Throkk]] and [[Polly]].

*"Why did God bless the prince?"* St. Joseph shouted across the clearing. [[Prince Krixen Ironfang]] looked back and kept walking.

A goblin child slipped a note into Polly's pocket: *meet at the northern tavern, next midnight.* The one behind him had not taken his eyes off Zaff.

> *"My name's Reddy Riz, great to meet you guys. My friends call me the Rizzler."*

[[Reddy Riz]] wanted to come along and showed them the butter knife he would protect himself with. Polly gave him a mechanical caterpillar and he vanished into the bushes with it. Throkk, at 18 hit points, turned down St. Joseph's potion because he had *heard terrible things about your potions* and walked south alone. The forest was quiet now and let him.

## The heroes of Ilyara

They reached [[Ilyara]] at sunrise to sprites and gnomes throwing petals and calling them heroes. And [[Enya]] walked out of the brothel.

> *"I was obviously a customer. My gold is gone."*

Her hand then found its way into Zaff's pocket, and a twist of luck he carries now caught it there. She was learning about the culture of the city, she said. [[Lola Greenling]], an elf innkeeper, gave them all rooms at the [[Petal and Pitcher]] for free, and they slept until the afternoon.

## The sword collects

[[The Ember Sword]] sent its bill when Throkk woke. He had lit it fourteen times and never once to kill, and he watched the life drain out of his body into the blade until the hilt burned bright again. Four points of Constitution, for a day. He kicked in the door of [[The Tomorrow Shop]].

> *"Why didn't you tell me that beforehand?"* *"Well, you didn't ask."*

Throkk asked [[Tallow]] why he had dropped the sword at the trial instead of killing Polly.

> *"Because my reputation of goodwill matters more to me than retribution."*

Throkk stepped up to intimidate him, tripped, and bloodied his nose on the counter. St. Joseph asked for a holy relic. Tallow had a hollow double turtle shell that a band of priests once swore was magic, and Enya felt in it the power of *what could be or once was*. Five hundred gold. Joseph had none.

## The disciples

So St. Joseph gathered the children who had followed the party all morning and told them God was asking each of them to reach their small hands into the pockets of the town. Ten went. They brought back 250 gold, and the Rizzler brought 100 on his own, beaming. One little girl picked the wrong pocket and came back with her face cut open. St. Joseph healed her. Her name is [[Maria]], and she has not left his side since.

## The council

[[Sein]] took Polly aside and told him that the moment Polly spoke into his mind was one of the biggest things to happen to the city in a decade. He tapped Polly's chest and the brand was gone. A beat later, the other voice.

> *"There. He took his hand off your leash, and you thanked him for it. Mine was never on you, little spark. I don't need one. You'll come to me."*

Throkk lit the ember sword in front of the council, and [[Maelis]] brought a root down on his hand. [[Lethiel]] told Polly about a satyr warlock named [[Bracken]], and blushed.

## Midnight at the Petal and Pitcher

A tall hooded man with a beautiful jawline stood, smiled, and embraced Polly.

> *"Oh, you must be Polly. Great to meet you. My name's Cassian."*

[[Cassian Vane]] wore a red cloak much like [[Seraveth]]'s, and he knew Enya's sceptre. The small hooded figure beside him was Krixen. St. Joseph got up and danced, glowing, the children dancing with him, until a lute came out and the noise covered the corner.

> *"I sat there for three days. My father, my brothers, they could care less. I'm done with this and I need some help."*

Krixen is the third son and now the eldest, and he wants his father, the Sovereign, gone. Zaff asked about a town ransacked some fifteen years ago, and learned that [[Varkesh]] keeps a library of every conquest it has made. Cassian offered two roads to an army, the mercenaries of [[Dur Soleth]] or the gnomes of [[Kharavel]], and paid seats on Krixen's council. Throkk skipped bail in Dur Soleth and the party killed its ruler's son. They chose the gnomes.

The voice again, in Polly's head: *They want your blade, but what else do they want?*

## One morning, three hours

Zaff said goodbye to [[Wix]].

> *"If I could have any wish, it's for you to be able to grow past this, like the trees of our forest, whose bark becomes new every year."*

Polly found [[Bracken]] dancing at the [[Root and Resin]] at half past ten in the morning, palmed two glasses of whatever black drink he was handed, and was his brother inside ten minutes. Bracken signed his pact in blood a decade ago and pays his patron in the love and lust other people feel for him. *Best ten years of my life.* Outside, Polly held out his hand and thought very hard. A small fart came out. Then the little wooden dragon on his belt started to move on its own.

St. Joseph sold his broom and five potions of flying to [[Fennel Underleaf]] and bought the turtle shell. Throkk called Fennel *Bill*, then *Dylan*, and showed him two eggs, which were seeds. Zaff's is worth hundreds. Throkk's is dead. He signed a partnership to plant both with a flat line, as *Throkk the orc*, and told Zaff it was the other way round.

## Four remain

Cassian was painting a teleportation circle outside the inn. Throkk grabbed his cloak and it was like grabbing steel. Enya studied the circle until she began to understand it, and Cassian tossed her a brush to finish it.

The disciples meant to come. Polly flew his dragon over their heads and one child chased it. Enya knelt and told them they reminded her of the children in her village. St. Joseph told the Rizzler he was his best disciple, and the Rizzler turned to the others like a quarterback in the fourth quarter. So Zaff cast guidance on Throkk, and Throkk raged and screamed at the children with spectral things howling around him.

Four stayed: the Rizzler, Maria, a gnome and a human. The circle closed on the smell of graphite, sulphur and iron oil, in front of the palace of [[Kharavel]]. A guard took one look and said *oh, fuck you again*, to Cassian.

## The Clockwork Cradle

[[Master Cobble Whistle-Gear]], Tick, knew Polly, and asked how Wallace was. *Wallace is dead.* Goblins. Tick looked at the two goblin children in the back of his shop.

> *"We might try to work with the goblins again."* *"What changed your mind?"* *"Maybe we met a good one."*

Polly's dragon flew for Tick, who had never made anything like it. Enya read it: not the magic she knows, something older and deeper, full of a passion she could not name. She told Polly less than she felt, and he could tell. Throkk bought a pogo stick that jumps a hundred feet, and a goblin child blew up an exploding chicken inside Tick's bottomless bag. Polly owes him two more.

## The feint

In the throne room the party introduced themselves.

> *"I'm Throkk. I used to be a farmer, and now I'm a murderer."*

[[King Orrin Delabre]] is too old for siege tents and wants a feint, his army marching on the Ironfang border to draw the goblin army out of Varkesh. [[Prince Werrin Delabre]] wants a siege and an untested aircraft. *"This is the perfect test, daddy."* [[High Marshal Toren Stannick]] raised his monocle and said *indubitably*. The party backed the feint.

The plan: blow the barracks, go over the walls, through the market and down under the keep, and hold the dam beneath [[Varkesh]]. Break the dam and the city sinks. Turn the wheel and the lava runs to Kharavel. Polly asked for ten thousand gold. The king offered two thousand, and the High Marshal's suggestion spell made it sound perfectly reasonable.

St. Joseph proposed his four disciples as a distraction, and as hostages. The Rizzler took out his butter knife.

> *"We will not have any child warriors on my battlefield."* *"God needs a distraction, and sometimes we have to use resources accordingly."*

## Where they stand

In the palace of [[Kharavel]], contracted for two thousand gold to open a dam under a goblin city. Cassian and Krixen will take the Sovereign. The party takes the dam. [[Throkk]] carries a sword that feeds on him, [[Polly]]'s dragon flies on its own, and [[St. Joseph]] has a turtle shell and four children who will not go home.
