The party loots the goblin cave, takes an escort job, and travels a long way west with two young women and a prophecy.

## The cave

The four of them found the hideout and went in hard.

They killed every goblin in it, room by room, and decapitated [[Skarnick]] at the end. Nobody tried to negotiate.

Then they stripped the place. They came away with a **Silver Gryphon Feather**, and [[Throkk]] found a biscuit that grants a bonus to his next persuasion attempt.

Then [[Polly]] did something nobody has fully explained. He dismembered [[Skarnick]]'s body, cut out his heart, and drove it into the wall of the cave.

A faint shudder ran through the whole cave at that moment.

## The ambush

Leaving the cave, the party was ambushed by [[Lyra Blackthorn]], [[Garin]] and [[Viola]], the same three adventurers from the inn, now working as bandits and trying to take [[Celeste Hawthorne]] for themselves.

**They had overheard [[Mayor Aldric Hawthorne]] offer the reward** outside the bar in [[Briarwood]]. They knew where she was and what she was worth, so they waited at the mouth of the cave and let the party do the work.

It went badly for them. [[Zaff]] wild shaped into a wolf and bit [[Garin]] in the neck, then spared his life in exchange for his iron sword. [[Lyra Blackthorn]] surrendered peacefully.

[[Viola]] lost an eye to [[Polly]] and got away clean. Nobody has seen her since.

Only [[Lyra Blackthorn]] was brought back to town.

## Back to Briarwood

The party hauled [[Lyra Blackthorn]] and [[Celeste Hawthorne]] home and put the whole thing in front of [[Mayor Aldric Hawthorne]]. The rescue, the ambush, and who had been waiting for them at the mouth of the cave.

[[Throkk]] argued that [[Lyra Blackthorn]] should be spared the stockades. The mayor agreed.

Then they negotiated. The reward on the table had only ever been part of the dowry, and the party pushed for the whole thing. Terms were struck: deliver [[Celeste Hawthorne]] to [[Aquitaine]] and collect in full.

They left with a payment note, a carriage and horses, and one extra passenger. [[Seraphine]], Celeste's childhood friend, was going too.

[[Briarwood]] and [[Aquitaine]] are a long way apart. Most of this session was the road.

## Junebug

One night on the road a young tabaxi girl walked into camp wearing a grey cloak she insisted was a superhero cape and carrying a stick she insisted was a magical sword.

[[Junebug]] asked every member of the party about their adventures and gave each of them a flattering nickname. She can turn pebbles into fireflies. [[Zaff]] gave her a slice of pie before she left.

## The water spirit

As the party approached [[Aquitaine]] a water spirit rose from the river, flooded the bridge to their ankles, and spoke a riddle. The full text is recorded in [[The Water Spirit's Riddle]].

[[St. Joseph]] hit it with sacred flame.

The spirit went into a rage, **swallowed the party's foal**, and shoved all of them into the river.

They went down it. They lost the carriage, took a beating on the way, and came ashore near a different entrance to the city entirely.

## The market

They walked up into [[Aquitaine]] soaked, on foot, without a carriage.

The city climbs in rings. Each walled circle sits inside the next and rises as it goes, so the whole place stacks upward toward the keep. Canals run through every level and most people move around by barge and raft. Fertile plains surround the city on every side, and the food grown on them moves through Aquitaine and out to the rest of the continent.

The route took them through the market, where they passed a goblin named [[Gritzzik]] selling his goods off a sheet spread on the ground. People were accusing him of stealing. He wants to open a shop and call it Gritzzik's Glorious Goods.

Somewhere in that crowd a child bumped into [[Zaff]] and dropped a coin into his pocket, engraved **BB**. The coin rode in that pocket, unnoticed, all the way into [[Session 03 - Beef's Beefy Doors]].

## Arrival

The party delivered [[Celeste Hawthorne]] and [[Seraphine]] to [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]] and his son [[Prince Caelen Tidelorn]].

Both men looked at the two young women and assumed [[Seraphine]] was the bride.

Nobody corrected them at first.

The Duke offered the party the stables to sleep in and free run of the keep for the rest of the day.
