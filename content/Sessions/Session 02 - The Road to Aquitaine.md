# Session 02: The Road to Aquitaine

The party loots the goblin cave, takes an escort job, and travels a long way west with two young women and a prophecy.

## The cave

The party stripped the hideout. They came away with a **Silver Gryphon Feather**, and [[Throkk]] found a biscuit that grants a bonus to his next persuasion attempt.

Then [[Polly]] did something nobody has fully explained. She dismembered [[Skarnick]]'s body, cut out his heart, and drove it into the wall of the cave.

A faint shudder ran through the whole cave at that moment.

## The ambush

Leaving the cave, the party was ambushed by [[Lyra Blackthorn]], [[Garin]] and [[Viola]], the same three adventurers from the inn, now working as bandits and trying to take [[Celeste Hawthorne]] for themselves.

It went badly for them. [[Garin]] took a wolf bite to the neck. [[Viola]] lost an eye to [[Polly]]. [[Lyra Blackthorn]] surrendered peacefully and was taken captive. [[Zaff]] spared [[Garin]]'s life in exchange for his iron sword.

Only [[Lyra Blackthorn]] was brought back to town. [[Throkk]] argued that she should be spared the stockades, and Mayor Aldric agreed.

## The escort

Terms were struck. The party would deliver [[Celeste Hawthorne]] to [[Aquitaine]] for the full dowry, and they left with a payment note, a carriage and horses, and one extra passenger: [[Seraphine]], Celeste's childhood friend.

[[Briarwood]] and [[Aquitaine]] are a long way apart. Most of this session was the road.

## Junebug

One night on the road a young tabaxi girl walked into camp wearing a grey cloak she insisted was a superhero cape and carrying a stick she insisted was a magical sword.

[[Junebug]] asked every member of the party about their adventures and gave each of them a flattering nickname. She can turn pebbles into fireflies. [[Zaff]] gave her a slice of pie before she left.

## The water spirit

As the party approached [[Aquitaine]] a water spirit rose from the river, flooded the bridge to their ankles, and spoke a riddle. The full text is recorded in [[The Water Spirit's Riddle]].

[[St. Joseph]] attacked it.

The spirit washed the entire party down the river. They lost the carriage, took damage, and came ashore near a different entrance to the city.

Somewhere in the chaos a child bumped into [[Zaff]] and left a coin in his pocket. It was engraved **BB**.

## Arrival

The party delivered [[Celeste Hawthorne]] and [[Seraphine]] to [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]] and his son [[Prince Caelen Tidelorn]].

Both men looked at the two young women and assumed [[Seraphine]] was the bride.

Nobody corrected them at first, and nobody has explained why they got it wrong.

The Duke offered the party the stables to sleep in and free run of the keep for the rest of the day.
