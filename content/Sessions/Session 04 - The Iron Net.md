[[Polly]] gropes a foreign dignitary, the party is sentenced to an escort job, and they cross the water on a ship called the Iron Net.

## The morning after

The party returned to [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]] late in the morning to collect the dowry.

A dignitary from [[Dur Soleth]], [[Son Soleth]], was at court. [[Polly]] grabbed his ass.

He was furious and demanded the Duke punish them.

The Duke's punishment was an assignment. The party would escort a caravan across the desert toward [[Dur Soleth]]. They did not have the standing to refuse, and that is how the party ended up on the road that changed the campaign.

## The wedding

[[Celeste Hawthorne]] stays in [[Aquitaine]]. She is betrothed to [[Prince Caelen Tidelorn]] and the wedding happens shortly. That was the entire point of hauling her across the country.

[[Seraphine]] stays with her.

## The docks

The night before departure the party went down to the harbour, and not to drink.

[[Prince Caelen Tidelorn]] has a reputation down there. He visits often, he sleeps around, and everybody working the waterfront knows it. The party went looking for someone willing to say so out loud.

**They were trying to dig up dirt on the prince to extort him with.**

It did not go well. They argued with the working women of the docks for most of the evening and could not get a single one of them to hand anything over. Not one useful name, not one usable night. Whatever the prince is doing down there, the people he does it with were not selling him to four strangers.

[[St. Joseph]] disappeared for a while during all this. He was found later. He has not been asked to elaborate.

They did get one thing out of the evening. [[Tobias]], a one-legged sailor with a thick Danish accent, offered them passage.

## The Iron Net

The ship was the **Iron Net**, under [[Captain Brannick Holt]], known as Red Knuckles.

They were on it a week.

The green went first. [[Aquitaine]] sits at the centre of fertile plains, and the further the Iron Net ran the drier the country on either bank got, until the farmland was gone and the party was looking at desert.

Then the water went. The Desert River thinned out and died, and the Iron Net could not go any further.

The crossing had not been quiet either. Electric eels hit the vessel on the way and the party fought them off in the water and over the rails.

They put ashore where the river ended, near the orc village, and started walking. Everything after this happens in [[The Desert Plains]].
