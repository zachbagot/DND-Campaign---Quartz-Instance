[[Polly]] gropes a foreign dignitary, the party is sentenced to an escort job, and they cross the water on a ship called the Iron Net.

## The morning after

The party returned to [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]] late in the morning to collect the dowry.

A dignitary from [[Dur Soleth]], [[Son Soleth]], was at court. [[Polly]] grabbed him by the backside.

He was furious and demanded the Duke punish them.

The Duke's punishment was an assignment. The party would escort a caravan across the plains toward [[Dur Soleth]]. They did not have the standing to refuse, and that is how the party ended up on the road that changed the campaign.

[[Celeste Hawthorne]] and [[Seraphine]] stayed behind in [[Aquitaine]]. They are still there.

## The docks

The night before departure the party went down to the harbour and found the company there. They drank and spent the evening in the usual way.

[[St. Joseph]] disappeared for a while. He was found later. He has not been asked to elaborate.

They also met [[Tobias]], a one-legged sailor with a thick Danish accent, who offered them passage.

## The Iron Net

The ship was the **Iron Net**, under [[Captain Brannick Holt]], known as Red Knuckles.

The crossing was not quiet. Electric eels attacked the vessel and the party fought them off in the water and over the rails.

They made landfall near the orc village on the far shore, and the plains road began.
