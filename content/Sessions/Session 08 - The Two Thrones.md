[[Polly]] is arrested thirty seconds after opening a shopkeeper from shoulder to hip, and the city that has no king puts him on trial for four murders he did not commit. By the end of the night a god has spoken over the forest and named three prophets, and one of them is a goblin prince.

[[Enya]] was not with the party. She was at the brothel.

## The flour

[[Polly]] never left [[The Tomorrow Shop]]. His cloak keeps him invisible even after he strikes from the shadows, so when the guards came they came blind, and they came prepared: [[Sein]] of the Council sealed the shop under a private sanctum that nothing physical or psychic could cross, then sent men in throwing **sacks of flour** through the air to find the shape of a man who was not there.

Polly went out a window on three clean rolls and opened a psychic whisper straight into Sein's head to tell him the shop was a bad place and he would not want it in his town.

> *"I'm assuming you're the one who just assaulted our shopkeeper. It is not up to you to decide if someone is in our city or not, let alone harm them and mutilate them like this. We are going to find you and you will be arrested and tried for your actions."*

Polly kept talking. Sein put dimensional shackles on him. [[Tallow]] was bleeding out in the street and a cleric got to him in time, and the first thing Tallow said when he could speak was *that's the guy.*

A bell rang across [[Ilyara]], and the whole city turned toward it and said the same word. *A trial.* One of them added: *I love a good execution.*

## Wix

The rest of the party was across the city meeting the woman who raised [[Zaff]].

[[Wix]] is a sprite, almost two hundred years old, and she is delighted by him and feeds him badly. She let him believe for years that she ran [[Ilyara]], and cheerfully corrected it: *"I am a big part of the sprites, but I don't run the city, no. I think I just have a big impact in your life, so you made some assumptions."*

Zaff told her he had come to take revenge on [[Varkesh]]. Very druidic revenge. Very healthy, naturey.

> *"I knew this day would come. I want you to know it wasn't just the goblins."*

Goblins were at [[Rootfield]]. That is where his eye went. But somebody else was there too, and Wix does not know who. There is one way to find out, and it is the road he was already on: **go to the goblins.** Not for vengeance.

> *"Zaff, the woods are alive, more than ever. And I don't know what's caused it. But now is not the time to make a fire."*

She named the five seats of [[The Council of Boroughs]] and marked their homes on the party's map. Then the bells started.

## Eye for an eye

[[Ilyara]] holds trials in an open amphitheatre under a tree the size of a tower. The tree is [[Maelis]].

> *"Welcome. As you might know, my name is Maelis and I've been here a long, long time. It's not a question of their innocence or guilty. It's a question of their consequence today."*

That is how justice works here. The accused does not argue that he did not do it. He argues that he should be allowed to live. Each of the five councillors holds a private reckoning that the accused never sees, and at the end they average it.

[[Brindle]], who holds the Thorn seat and the city's defence, ran the prosecution and wanted a death. The gallery agreed with him, loudly and in rhythm: **eye for an eye.**

The charges were four murders, one attempted murder, and damaging the canopy with a burning sword. Polly had done exactly one of those things.

[[Tallow]] testified from under his bandages. Then [[Throkk]] took the stand as a character witness and invented a saint:

> *"Polly is an eccentric man, I'll say that. He's known to touch a few asses. You know, every day like it could be his last. He takes his time to help the children. He sponsors multiple orphanages. He's a proficient toy maker, well known throughout the land."*

Asked to prove it, Polly produced a carved wooden dragon with a tail that moves. It was the best thing anyone in the amphitheatre had ever seen. [[Sein]] was won over on the spot.

Then [[St. Joseph]] was called, and a child tugged his cloak and asked whether he was religious or a mage.

> *"I'm a member of God. And every week on Sunday, Polly would come in the church, give tithings to the church. And twenty years ago, before I met Polly, the children, we would use child labor. And once Polly came to the town, we had our designated chimney sweeper, so we could abolish child labor."*

Every word of that is now true. [[Polly]] ended child labour in a town by climbing the chimneys himself. He is a hero. He mentioned quietly afterwards that he has never been to church a day in his life.

[[Calico]], a satyr, testified that her brother walked into the woods the day before and came back opened the same way Tallow was. [[Aster]] and [[Sylvia]] testified that they had personally flown the party in from the southwest that same morning, which meant Polly had been in the city for a matter of hours and the killings had been going on for months.

And then a mage the party had not seen since the desert walked through the door.

## Liss

[[Liss]] ran east after the orcs released her, away from the [[Death Rattles|caravan]] and away from anyone who might ask where she had been. She had been in [[Ilyara]] a few days keeping her head down when she heard a gnome was on trial for his life.

> *"They did get me out of some tough situations. I can definitely speak for their character. We don't have a lot of experience, but they did save my life. So I will absolutely say that there is good in them. All of them."*

[[Polly]] bowed to her so low his beard touched the ground.

## The roots

Something interrupted the trial.

The ground shook. [[Maelis]], [[Lethiel]] and [[Brindle]] looked at each other, and the roots under the amphitheatre floor began to braid themselves together into two cords as thick as a man's arm, and those cords went straight for [[Zaff]].

The three eldest beings in the room watched it happen and did not move.

[[Throkk]] caught the vines in his bare hands and held on. Zaff, wrapped and being dragged, called out to the council to explain what they clearly already understood, and started to cast. He never finished it. **The vines felt what he intended and let go**, so suddenly that Throkk was nearly pulled off his feet.

The council conferred. [[Brindle]] asked whether they were sure they could not just kill him. They were sure.

> *"It is clear that this one man, annoying and arrogant as he is, couldn't have killed all four of these people. It is also clear that the forest has not been itself in recent months, and we don't know why. So if you want to ever step foot here again and not be permanently scarred, we need to send you to the forest and you figure that out for us. Because clearly, one of you has something to do with this. Or maybe all of you."*

Not acquitted. Not condemned. Conscripted.

## The blade Tallow would not take

The crowd was still chanting when [[Brindle]] drew his sword, walked it across the floor, and put it in [[Tallow]]'s hands.

A guard kicked [[Polly]]'s legs out so he was kneeling. Tallow came and stood over him with the sword, and the whole amphitheatre was screaming for it.

He looked Polly in the eyes. He dropped the sword. He walked out.

Nobody explained it, and nobody has seen him since.

[[Sein]] pressed a mark into Polly's chest, something like a tattoo, visible as a glimmer through an open shirt. It is not decoration and it is not a mystery: **it is a brand, and it means Polly cannot run.** He goes north and settles what is wrong with the forest, or Sein kills him. The shackles came off because the brand replaced them.

## The voice

As they were leaving, something spoke inside [[Polly]]'s head that nobody else could hear.

> *"Look at how small they want you to be. They tell you to temper your fury. They tell you to swallow your grief. They want a polite, quiet, and simple little gnome. But I taste what burns inside you. A little spark, your rage, your hunger. Give your passions to me, and I will make sure you never, ever feel helpless again."*

He could not tell whether it came from outside him or from his own head. Whatever it was, it was enjoying itself.

## The dark

A boy caught [[St. Joseph]] before they reached the treeline and asked him to say something nice to make him stronger. St. Joseph said a Hail Mary over him, and the blessing settled on the entire party.

Past the treeline it was full night under a canopy so thick the full moon vanished. Vines underfoot at every step, and when [[Liss]] studied them she found they are **connected to one another and share what they know**. Zaff spoke to a tree and the tree said nothing back. He asked the rabbits instead, and a rabbit pointed north.

The forest itself started killing them. Cold air thick with black mould, breath coated in grease, boots sinking into rot, and a weight that pulled at the shoulders and would not stop coming back. [[Throkk]] said the quiet part out loud somewhere in there: this forest has made me aware of how long we take.

Four blights dropped out of the trees. Then a field of mushrooms that detonate, which Throkk simply ran through. Then two wood rot trolls, thirty feet apart, flanking, each one leaking a necrotic rot that ate anything standing near it.

That fight nearly ended the campaign. [[St. Joseph]] summoned the archangel Michael and levitated out of a floor of wooden spikes on two natural twenties. [[Liss]] threw lightning down the line between both trolls. [[Zaff]] burned a moonbeam and a ring charge to stay alive. [[Throkk]] went reckless, at 12 hit points, and cleaved the last one down. Everyone was exhausted past the point of standing.

And then the forest went quiet, and there was firelight ahead.

## The two thrones

Fifty goblins in a clearing, and it is not a camp so much as a vigil.

Torches. Drummers at the back keeping up music. An encampment thrown together around one chair, and around the chair a **harem of women**, some of them openly crying. At the centre of them, on a carved throne, a young goblin in a crown, **entombed in vines to the forehead**, vines pressed against his face and his arms, the crown sitting awkwardly on top of the growth. One of the women is feeding him porridge a spoonful at a time.

He had been in that chair for three days, and he had walked into it on his own feet. They are mourning him because to them he is already dead.

> *"It needs two. It always needed two. I sat because my people were dying in the dark, but the wood screams for the other half. If you run, little one, our children pay for our cowardice. Please sit with me and let the bleeding stop."*

Facing his chair, across the clearing, was a second throne. Empty.

[[Zaff]] had gone into his starry form and the moon was on him, and as he stepped into the clearing [[St. Joseph]] snuffed every goblin torch at once with a word, so that the only thing left to look at in the whole dark forest was the glowing druid walking to the empty chair.

He asked what caused this before he took his place. **[[Prince Krixen Ironfang]]** told him: the ancient contract was broken. Their lore says it breaks when a goblin or a gnome is desecrated.

Zaff sat down and let the vines take him.

They wrapped him, then burrowed into him, and his blood ran up into the green until the vines turned red and pushed something back into him in exchange. The prince started screaming. So did the forest.

## The one true god

Then the sky broke open.

A great boom went across it like an explosion, sound waves shaking the trees themselves, **and with it a vast blast of light that lit the whole forest at once.** It was heard in [[Ilyara]] and in [[Varkesh]] both, and in Ilyara they saw it too, the forest to their north going white for a moment under a canopy that had been swallowing the full moon all night.

> *"My child, you have willingly sacrificed yourself for the nature. I have watched you for your entire life. And time and time again you have proven yourself to be one with this earth. And so I call upon you, Zaff, to become the prophet of the earth."*
>
> *"And then you, Prince Krixen, I call you to be prophet of the metals."*

And then it turned, and lit up [[St. Joseph]], who had not asked for anything.

> *"You have surprised me more than all. For time and time again, you could attack this world's creatures, but not once have you struck one down in all of your trials. So you shall be the prophet of the air and the sound."*

Eight sessions. Nobody at the table had noticed, and it was true.

> *"I am the one true God, and you three shall be my prophets."*

[[St. Joseph]] said *I don't accept false deities*, and was told, patiently, that this was not a false deity, and then accepted. [[Zaff]] accepted too, and knew for certain the voice believed every word it said.

Fifty goblins watched it happen. [[Prince Krixen Ironfang]] was let go, and stood up for the first time in three days, very weak. The harem that had been weeping over him swarmed him, hugging and kissing him.

## Xeraf

While the goblins were still on their knees, an older goblin in military dress walked up to [[Zaff]].

Zaff knew him immediately. He is the reason [[Rootfield]] is a ruin and the reason there is an eyepatch.

> *"That's Xeraf?"* — *"Yes. The one and only."*

[[Xeraf]] did not know him back.

> *"You don't remember the town?"* — *"I've been to many towns."*
> *"The town you've burned?"* — *"I've burned many towns."*

Zaff lifted the patch and showed him the necrotic eye underneath.

> *"Look, I've seen many missing eyes. I'm sorry you're missing your eye. I don't know you though."*

A hundred people, a town gathering food for winter, a boy of eight used as bait to draw the defenders out and then left in the ruins without food or water. Xeraf called it self-defence, and retribution for raiders, and said that now was not the time because this is a holy place. He called his own prince beneath him. Then he laughed, and turned, and walked away with his soldiers.

Zaff put a hand on his shoulder as he went.

> *"This will be the last time this is a peaceful attempt at communication."*

## Where they stand

In a clearing in the middle of a forest that has stopped screaming, in front of fifty goblins and the man who burned [[Zaff]]'s village. Zaff is bound to the gnome throne with plant matter inside him and a god's title on him. [[Prince Krixen Ironfang]] is free and carries one too. So does [[St. Joseph]], who never asked. [[Polly]] carries a mark from [[Sein]] that nobody has explained and a voice in his head that will not go away. [[Liss]] travels with them now. All of them are hurt, and the exhaustion has stopped climbing but has not lifted.
