*The Petal and Pitcher in the morning mist.*

An inn at the northern edge of [[Ilyara]], the first thing the party saw walking back in out of the forest. Run by [[Lola Greenling]]. Lively, full of every race in the city, with a lute when the night calls for one.

The party slept there for free in [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]]. At midnight [[Cassian Vane]] and [[Prince Krixen Ironfang]] were waiting in the far right corner, and [[St. Joseph]] danced, glowing, with a crowd of children, until the noise covered the whole conversation. Cassian painted his teleportation circle on the grass outside its front door.
