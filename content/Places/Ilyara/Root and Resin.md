The worst part of [[Ilyara]], down at the root of the great flower where no sunlight reaches, and the tavern that gives it its name. Home to the city's darkest and most criminal citizens.

When [[Polly]] walked in at half past ten in the morning in [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]], the regulars had been there all night, gambling at blackjack by different rules. One very pale man at the card table had his hand up and was missing two fingers. [[Bracken]] was on the dance floor. Polly changed nothing about himself and fit right in.
