*The Clockwork Cradle, hanging off the chasm wall above a rope bridge.*

A toymakers' and craftsmen's shop cut into the rock of [[Kharavel]]'s chasm, in the middle tier of the city. Run by [[Master Cobble Whistle-Gear]], whom everyone calls Tick, and full of clockwork birds, clocks and toys made of rock, wood and brass. [[Polly]] has known it for decades.

The party spent an hour there in [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]] waiting to be received at the palace. Tick showed them a bird that flies for as long as a mirror's beam is on it, asked Polly about Wallace, and had never seen anything like Polly's wooden dragon, which flies with no light at all. [[Throkk]] bought a pogo stick that jumps a hundred feet for 45 gold and a good review. A goblin child knocked over an exploding chicken on the way out and it went off inside Tick's bottomless bag, so Polly owes him two exploding chickens.
