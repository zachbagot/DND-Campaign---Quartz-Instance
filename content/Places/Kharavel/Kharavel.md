The city of mirrors, north of the desert in the mountains. Named by [[Eugene Johnson]] in [[Session 07 - The Druid's Ruins]] as one of the two great cities within reach, the other being [[Ilyara]].

![[Kharavel.png]]
*The city of mirrors, homes cut into the chasm walls all the way down.*

The party arrived in [[Session 09 - It's in God's Hands]], teleported by [[Cassian Vane]], two weeks' walk covered in a moment. The air smells of graphite, sulphur and iron oil.

**A city built into the walls of a chasm**, thousands of homes cut into the rock, bridges across the gap. Sunlight barely reaches the bottom, so great mirrors along the rim throw light down into the homes to grow their plants, and the families who rent out the mirrors are the city's upper class. Paper blimps drift across the chasm, lifted by water heated with mirror light, some civil, some scientific, some military. Once lava from [[Varkesh]] ran through the chasm and fed the city's forges. Now it is a weak glow at the very bottom, and [[Polly]], who is from here, promises it is usually much bigger.

![[Kharavel Palace.png]]
*The palace on the rim, looking out over the chasm where the lava comes down from the far side of the mountain.*

The palace stands on the rim of the chasm and overlooks it, and the lava that once fed the city ran down into the chasm from the far side of the mountain. The city is ruled by [[King Orrin Delabre]], with his heir [[Prince Werrin Delabre]] and [[High Marshal Toren Stannick]] at his side. [[Master Cobble Whistle-Gear]] runs [[The Clockwork Cradle]], a toymakers' shop in the middle tier.
