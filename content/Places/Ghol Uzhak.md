The old den. A pocket dimension reached through a lake in orc country, holding a village built around a single colossal tusk. No outsider had been brought inside in ten thousand years before [[Throkk]], [[Polly]], [[Zaff]], [[St. Joseph]] and [[Enya]] were brought in by [[Vorrak Stonebell]]. See [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]] for what happened there.

## Getting in

You walk into the water of the lake. Then you are looking up at water.

Ghol Uzhak sits between spatial planes. The ceiling of the world is the surface of the lake seen from underneath, and the sun still comes through it, so the whole village is lit from above by daylight filtered through moving water.

The shore of the lake on the outside holds a small shanty settlement. That is where prisoners are kept, including [[Captain Aldric Renn]].

## The tusk

One tusk. Roughly 200 feet across, 150 feet tall, curving, with a diameter of about 30 feet. It is hollow enough that people live inside it.

Rough scaffolding runs up the outside like a set of stairs. Netting is strung underneath, between the tusk, the ground, and the ceiling of water.

The children's game is to run the scaffolding, jump, reach up and touch the underside of the water, and drop back down. Visitors who manage it get applause.

**Nobody knows what it came from.** The understanding among the orcs is that there were gigantic beasts in the world before the current peoples, and that they died out as more intelligent creatures prospered. The tusk is what one of them left behind.

## The fire

At the base of the tusk. Older than the world by the reckoning of the people who keep it, and it is a real magic rather than a symbol. It is where the branding happens.

[[Yagra Emberveil]] keeps it. She says it does not choose, that she is only the hand and the fire is the eye, and that it marks what a person actually is rather than what they show people.

She told the party it had been expecting them, and did not say what for.

## Who lives here

- **[[Yagra Emberveil]]**, shaman and keeper of the fire. Ancient, small for an orc, bent like a root, both eyes clouded white, and she tracks people precisely anyway.
- **Brakka Two-Hammers**, blacksmith at the Silt Forge. Enormous and cheerful.
- **Hesk**, tanner at the Second Skin. Very old, nearly deaf.
- **Miv**, Hesk's granddaughter, nine years old and fearless.
- **Senna Reedmane**, at the Kelp Paddock, who matches riders to giant marsh striders.
- **Vorrak Stonebell** is a guest here rather than a resident, and he is the one who brought the party in.
