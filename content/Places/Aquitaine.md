A large city built in concentric rings, ruled by [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]] with his son [[Prince Caelen Tidelorn]] as heir. Sometimes called Aqualune.

It is a long way west of [[Briarwood]], and the party spent most of [[Session 02 - The Road to Aquitaine]] getting there.

## The city

Rings inside rings. Each outer circle surrounds the next, walled, and the city climbs as it goes inward, so the rings rise into the sky one behind another and the inner keep sits highest of all behind the heaviest walls.

Water runs through all of it. Canals thread every ring, and most people get around by barge or raft rather than on foot.

**Aquitaine feeds the continent.** Fertile plains surround the city on every side, and it is the bread basket of the kingdom. Food grown on those plains moves through Aquitaine and out to the rest of the continent.

## Who really runs it

[[Beef the Bard]] does. The city has a Duke, a keep and a court, and the thieves guild is what people actually answer to. Her front is **Beef's Beefy Doors**. See [[Session 03 - Beef's Beefy Doors]].

## Notable

- The keep, where [[Celeste Hawthorne]] and [[Seraphine]] were delivered, and where the party slept in the stables.
- **Traveler's Trove General Shop**, owned by Alden Merrik, robbed by the party on Beef's instructions.
- An orphanage [[Polly]] helped on an earlier visit, where [[Thrain Battleborn]] volunteers.
- The market, where [[Gritzzik]] the goblin sells his goods off a sheet on the ground.
- The docks, where the party met [[Tobias]] and boarded the Iron Net.

[[Celeste Hawthorne]] and [[Seraphine]] are both still here.
