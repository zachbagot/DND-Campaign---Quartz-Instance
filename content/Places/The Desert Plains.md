Arid, dry country, and the setting of [[Session 05 - The Caravan]] and [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]].

A separate region from the fertile plains that surround [[Aquitaine]]. The two run alongside each other and do not blend. You cross from one into the other.

The party reached it by water. The Iron Net carried them a week up the Desert River, out of the green country around [[Aquitaine]] and into the dry, until the river thinned out and died. Where it ended, the marching started.

Chulls pull the caravan wagons across it. Elder Bog Ashveil rides one at the front of the column.

There is no shade out here and water is a fixed quantity you carry. That is the whole reason the march killed people.

The relocation itself traces back to [[Throkk]]. He killed human tax collectors in his village years ago, the humans answered with a garrison, and the escalation ran from there to the column.

## The ford

One of the few places on the route with real water in it. [[Vorrak Stonebell]] set his ambush at the crossing, and the fighting there became the **Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge**.

## The marsh

Three days east of the ford, under a marsh that appears on no map, is the lake that leads to [[Ghol Uzhak]].

A wetland in the middle of dry country, which nobody has commented on.

A small shanty settlement sits on its shore, outside the pocket dimension, and that is where prisoners are held. [[Captain Aldric Renn]] is there now.
