A quiet town with an inn called the **Rusty Tankard**, run by [[Tobin]]. It is where the campaign started and it no longer exists.

In [[Session 01 - The Burning of Briarwood]] a gigantic red dragon tore the roof off the inn, looked around as though searching for something, dropped a horde of goblins and a single werewolf, and left. The town was destroyed in the fighting that followed.

## Who was there

- **Mayor Aldric**, a tall heavy human in his forties with speckled white and brown hair and skin leathered by years in the sun. His daughter [[Celeste Hawthorne]] was taken by the goblins.
- [[Tobin]], the innkeeper, who fought alongside the party.
- [[Maris]], an alchemist, with her cat Tobby. The party never spoke to her.
- [[Seraphine]], her daughter.
- **Gritzzik**, a goblin who sold goods off a sheet on the ground, got accused of stealing by the townsfolk, and wanted to open a shop called Gritzzik's Glorious Goods.
- [[Lyra Blackthorn]], [[Garin]] and [[Viola]], drinking at the inn.
- **A tall redheaded mage**, seen briefly, who teleported away. Never identified.

## Unanswered

The dragon was looking for something. It did not find it, and it left.
