The city that governs the plains, and the destination the caravan was marching toward in [[Session 05 - The Caravan]].

**The party has never been there.** They turned on the caravan at the ford in [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]] and went east to [[Ghol Uzhak]] instead.

What they know about it:

- [[Son Soleth]], the dignitary [[Polly]] groped at [[Duke Thalorien Tidelorn]]'s court, is from there.
- [[Throkk]] posted ten silver in bail on the march with an expectation to appear at trial in Dur Soleth. He has not appeared.
- The orcs on the march were being taken there, though not into the city itself.
