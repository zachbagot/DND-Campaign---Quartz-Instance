A ring worn by [[Zaff]]. Taken at the end of [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]].

## What it does

- Once per long rest, cast **cure wounds at 2nd level** without expending a spell slot.

## What it looks like

A silver band wound with living green. **It blooms one small white flower each dawn.**

## Where it came from

Off the hand of a human field-medic.

He died dragging an orc child to cover.

He was on the other side. He was wearing a ring that heals, and he spent the end of his life carrying a child of the people his column was marching to an internment quarter, and the ring came off his body and onto the hand of an aasimar druid the fire had just marked with a seed growing out of ash.

It still flowers every morning.
