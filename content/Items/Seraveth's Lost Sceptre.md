A **+2 spellcasting focus**, carried by [[Enya]]. Taken at the end of [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]].

## What it does

- **+2** to spell attack rolls and save DC.
- Once per long rest, either add **1d4 to your save DC** for one casting, or **upcast one spell a level higher for free**.

## Where it came from

Found beneath a dead war-mage. **His hands were burned where he had gripped it**, as though it had refused him.

## Whose it is

The make matches [[Seraveth]]'s known work.

Which leaves two answers, and neither is comfortable. Either it was stolen from the Mage Warden's faction, or somebody inside that faction handed it to the man who died holding it.

[[Seraveth]] fled the ridge at one hit point and one arm lighter. The party has the arm. Now they have this as well.

## What happened when Enya picked it up

It did not burn her.

It settled.

Like it recognised the blood.
