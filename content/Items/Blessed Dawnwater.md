A potion carried by [[St. Joseph]]. Taken at the end of [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]].

## What it does

- Heals **4d4+4**.
- The drinker **sheds bright light for one hour** afterward.

## What it is

Blessed water, glowing faintly gold, brewed for medics who work at night. The light is the point. A healer who drinks it becomes a lamp, and everyone bleeding within reach can see where to crawl.

Drinking it feels like dawn arriving early.
