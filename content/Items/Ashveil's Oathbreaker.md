A **+2 greataxe**, carried by [[Throkk]]. Taken at the end of [[Session 06 - The Battle of Blood Gulch Ridge]].

## What it does

- **+2** to attack and damage rolls.
- While raging, resistance to being **frightened**.
- It **hums** near anything tied to the [[Death Rattles]].

## Where it came from

Pried from a fallen orc champion who had been pressed into the ambushers' service.

Old **Ashveil** clan-marks have been filed off the blade. Not completely. The grooves still catch the light, and they still hold the shape of a name.

## The name in the grooves

Elder Bog Ashveil rode with the caravan. So did his daughter Grutha.

Somebody took an Ashveil champion, put him in service, and then took the trouble to file his family off his weapon. They did not finish the job.

Nobody has asked Bog Ashveil about it yet.
